Branch ages: YFull against FTDNA Discover #

Branches with the same name in both trees: 19,086. For each, the age of the common ancestor (TMRCA) estimated by YFull is compared with FTDNA's estimate. On average YFull's estimate is 7% younger; YFull gives a younger age for 68% of branches. For 98% of branches the two estimates stay within each other's margin of error. The date a branch split from its parent (YFull's formed) is not compared here.

How deep into the tree YFull samples and FTDNA kits reach #

YFull samples are placed on the FTDNA tree, so depth can be compared directly. In the typical one of 67 countries a YFull sample sits 37.0 branchings from the root, an FTDNA kit 31.5. 34% of YFull samples reached the finest branch, against 24% of FTDNA kits. That is why the same country looks different at the two companies: YFull's whole-genome tests go deeper. On the maternal line there is no gap: in the typical one of 28 countries the depth differs by only −0.1 branchings, against +4.5 for Y-DNA. Both companies read the whole mitochondrial DNA.
