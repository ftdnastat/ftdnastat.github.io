Y × mt: how much of the link is geography #

Mutual information between a person's paternal and maternal lines over 10,473 YFull pairs with a known country (40 countries with 80+ pairs), minus the same on shuffled pairs. Undivided 0.41 bits; within countries 0.08; on the same pairs with countries shuffled at random 0.11 — splitting alone eats part of the signal. Against that honest baseline the country explains 27% of the link. The region (2,259 pairs in 24 regions with 50+ pairs) — 51%: the finer the geography, the more of the link it takes; the rest is structure inside regions, not kinship of pairs.

How the link vanishes inside a country

  • J1-L858 × R0a: worldwide ×6.1 (194), inside the country (Saudi Arabia) ×1.1
  • J1-L858 × J1d: worldwide ×5.2 (44), inside the country (Saudi Arabia) ×1.1
  • N-Z1936 × U5b: worldwide ×4.2 (42)
