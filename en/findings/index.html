Paternal and maternal lines: are they linked, and how much of it is just geography #

Both lines are known for one person, and they are not independent: knowing the paternal line lets you guess something about the maternal one. The link was measured over 10,630 YFull pairs with a known country (40 countries with 80+ pairs). Knowing only a person's country explains 0% of the whole link between their lines. The region explains even more, 27% (2,389 pairs in 26 regions with 50+ pairs). The finer the geography, the more of the link turns out to be simply “people from the same place”. What remains is structure inside regions, not kinship of the pairs themselves.
