How to read the cards

For Russia, FTDNA lets testers pick only the republics; oblasts and krais are not on its list.

Y: Main branches of the Y tree. Each branch counts every kit in it and in its sub-branches; sub-branches are indented. Age is how many years ago the branch's common ancestor lived (TMRCA).

mt: Main mt-tree branches grouped by letter, sub-branches indented. Each branch counts every kit in it and in its sub-branches. Age is how many years ago the branch's common foremother lived, from FTDNA data where available.

A colour marks one of the eight most common macro-haplogroups (the haplogroup's first letter); everything else is grey.RIJENGQTother
