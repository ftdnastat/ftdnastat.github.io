Lombards

The Lombards were a Germanic people who, according to Roman authors, lived near the mouth of the Elbe in the 1st–2nd centuries CE. In the 5th century they moved to the Middle Danube region. In 568, under King Alboin, they left Pannonia and took possession of Italy, where their kingdom lasted until 774. A DNA study of two cemeteries in Hungary and northern Italy associated with the Lombards showed that each was organized around one large pedigree and included groups of different ancestry with distinct funerary customs.

Sources: Лангобарды — Википедия; Lombards — Wikipedia; Amorim C. E. G. et al. (2018). Understanding 6th-century barbarian social organization and migration through paleogenomics. Nature Communications
