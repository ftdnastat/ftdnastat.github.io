Xiongnu

The Xiongnu were nomads of antiquity who lived in the steppes north of China from 220 BCE to the 2nd century CE. Constant wars with the Han empire welded them into a single power to which the neighboring nomadic tribes submitted. Its lands stretched from Manchuria to the Pamirs and covered most of Mongolia and southern Siberia. Historians have not agreed on where the Xiongnu came from. The genomes of 18 individuals from elite cemeteries on the western edge of the empire show that a single community was genetically almost as diverse as the empire as a whole, and that people of the lowest status differed the most.

Sources: Хунну — Википедия; Xiongnu — Wikipedia; Lee J. et al. (2023). Genetic population structure of the Xiongnu Empire at imperial and local scales. Science Advances
