Gravettian

The Gravettian is an archaeological industry of the European Upper Palaeolithic that succeeded the Aurignacian around 33,000 years ago. It had mostly disappeared by about 22,000 years ago, close to the Last Glacial Maximum, although some elements lasted until about 17,000 years ago. It was first identified at La Gravette in the Dordogne department of southwestern France and is known for its art, notably Venus figurines carved from ivory or limestone. Ancient DNA suggests it was produced by several genetically divergent groups of hunter-gatherers: the Věstonice cluster in the east and the Fournol cluster in the west. In Portugal, Spain and France it was succeeded by the Solutrean.

Sources: Граветтская культура — Википедия; Gravettian — Wikipedia
