Grossgartach culture

The Grossgartach culture was an archaeological culture of the Middle Neolithic that flourished around 4750/4650 BCE. It spanned the middle Rhine and upper Rhine regions, including Alsace, southern Germany, and northern and northeastern Switzerland. It was succeeded by the Rössen culture between 4600 and 4300 BCE, and is considered part of the larger Rössen complex of cultures. Alfred Schliz named it after a site excavated at Grossgartach, now part of the municipality of Leingarten in the Heilbronn district.

Sources: Гроссгартахская культура — Википедия; Grossgartach culture — Wikipedia
