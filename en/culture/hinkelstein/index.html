Hinkelstein culture

The Hinkelstein culture is a Neolithic archaeological culture situated in the Rhine-Main area and Rhenish Hesse in Germany. It is a megalithic culture, part of the wider Linear Pottery horizon, dating to approximately the 50th to 49th century BCE. The name was suggested by Karl Koehl of Worms in 1900. Hinkelstein is the local Hessian dialect word for menhir, after a menhir discovered in 1866 in Monsheim. Hinkel is a Hessian term for chicken, and the standard German name for menhirs, Hünenstein, or giants' stone, was sometimes jokingly turned into Hühnerstein, chicken-stone.
