Mycenaean Greece

The Mycenaean civilization was the final stage of the Bronze Age in Greece, in the 16th–11th centuries BCE. It is named after Mycenae in the Argolid, on the Peloponnese; other major centers included Argos, Tiryns and Pylos. The Mycenaeans fought and conquered, but they also traded across the Mediterranean. The end of their culture is linked to the Bronze Age collapse and the Dorian invasion around 1200 BCE. According to paleogenetics, the Mycenaeans were genetically close to the Minoans but carried additional ancestry from a source related to the hunter-gatherers of Eastern Europe and Siberia.

Sources: Микенская цивилизация — Википедия; Mycenaean Greece — Wikipedia; Lazaridis I. et al. (2017). Genetic origins of the Minoans and Mycenaeans. Nature
