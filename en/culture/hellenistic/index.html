Hellenistic period

The Hellenistic period covers the time in Greek, Eastern Mediterranean and West Asian history between the death of Alexander the Great in 323 BCE and the death of Cleopatra VII in 30 BCE, when these regions were under the influence of Greek leadership, culture and language. After Alexander's conquests and the Wars of the Diadochi, Greek kingdoms arose across West Asia, Northeast Africa and South Asia, among them the Seleucid Empire, the Ptolemaic Kingdom and the Greco-Bactrian Kingdom. The resulting fusion of Greek and local cultures gave rise to Koine Greek, which became the lingua franca of the ancient world. The era was marked by prosperity and progress in the arts, literature, philosophy and science.

Sources: Эллинизм — Википедия; Hellenistic period — Wikipedia
