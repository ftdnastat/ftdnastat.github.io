Monteoru culture

The Monteoru culture was a Bronze Age archaeological culture of Romania and Moldova, dating from about 2400 BCE to the 16th century BCE. It derived from the preceding Glina-Schneckenberg culture, was succeeded by the Noua-Sabatinovka culture, and was contemporary with the related Tei culture. The name comes from an ancient settlement site at Sărata-Monteoru in Buzău County. Its earliest phases, around 2400–2250 BCE, favoured inhumation in stone cist graves, a trait shared with contemporary neighbouring groups. Excavations in 1955–1956 uncovered a necropolis with over a thousand cremation burials.

Sources: Монтеору — Википедия; Monteoru culture — Wikipedia
