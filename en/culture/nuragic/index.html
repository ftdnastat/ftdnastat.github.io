Nuragic civilization

The Nuragic civilization formed on the Mediterranean island of Sardinia in the Bronze Age and lasted from the 18th century BC until the Iron Age or the start of Roman colonization in 238 BC. By the traditional theory of Giovanni Lilliu, it developed after migrations from the west of people related to the Beaker culture, while other scholars suggest a local origin. Its name comes from the nuraghe, a tower-fortress that the ancient Sardinians built in large numbers from about 1800 BC; more than 7,000 still dot the island. No written records have been found apart from a few possible short inscriptions from its last stages, so the only written information comes from Greek and Roman authors.

Sources: Нураги — Википедия; Nuragic civilization — Wikipedia
