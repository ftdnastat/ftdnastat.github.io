Sagsai culture

The Sagsai culture (1500–1000 BCE) was an early Bronze Age culture of Western Mongolia. It was centred on the Sagsai area, the westernmost region of modern Mongolia, and succeeded the Chemurchek culture. Its people led a pastoral lifestyle, raising sheep, cattle and horses. The oldest Sagsai sites lie in the Altai region, from where the culture later expanded into western Mongolia. Dated petroglyphs suggest that it used horse chariots before 1200 BCE, and it was followed by the Deer stones culture.
