Urartu

Urartu was an ancient state of Western Asia in the Armenian Highlands; in the first quarter of the 1st millennium BCE it was the strongest in the region. The first documentary evidence of Urartu, then still a union of tribes, dates to the 13th century BCE; as a state it is attested from the 9th century BCE, and in the 6th century BCE it disappeared. In the first half of the 8th century BCE Urartu prevailed over its constant rival, Assyria. Ancient DNA shows that people of the kingdom's core around Lake Van lacked the steppe ancestry common in the northern provinces.

Sources: Урарту — Википедия; Urartu — Wikipedia; Lazaridis I. et al. (2022). A genetic probe into the ancient and medieval history of Southern Europe and West Asia. Science
