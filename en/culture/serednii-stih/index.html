Sredny Stog culture

The Sredny Stog culture is a pre-Kurgan archaeological culture of the mid-5th to mid-4th millennia BC. It is named after the Dnieper islet of Serednii Stih in Ukraine, where it was first located. It was a Chalcolithic culture of the Black Sea steppes, stretching from the Danube delta in the west to the Dnieper, the upper Donets, the lower Don and the Sea of Azov in the east. Known settlements include Molyukhov Bugor, Alexandria and Deriivka, and the burials are single inhumations on the back with bent legs, often stained with red ochre. Its people mainly kept livestock, probably including early forms of horse breeding. Two main periods are distinguished: an early pre-Corded one and a late Corded, or Deriivka, one.

Sources: Среднестоговская культура — Википедия; Sredny Stog culture — Wikipedia
