Göktürks

The Göktürks, also called Türks, Celestial Turks or Blue Turks, were a Turkic people of medieval Inner Asia and the dominant power of the Turkic Khaganate. They arose from a mixing of the local Altai population with the incoming Ashina clan. Under Bumin Qaghan, who died in 552, and his sons they succeeded the Rouran Khaganate as the main power of the region and founded the First Turkic Khaganate. It was one of several nomadic dynasties that shaped the future geography, culture and dominant beliefs of the Turkic peoples. The Turkic ethnos formed among mixed Caucasoid-Mongoloid types, so on its westward spread it carried features of both.

Sources: Древние тюрки — Википедия; Göktürks — Wikipedia
