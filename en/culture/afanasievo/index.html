Afanasievo culture

The Afanasievo culture was an Eneolithic culture of southern Siberia (3300–2500 BCE). Its core is the Altai and the Minusinsk Basin, but sites are also known in eastern Kazakhstan, western Mongolia and Xinjiang. The Afanasievo people kept cattle, horses and sheep without giving up hunting and fishing, and were the first in Siberia to bury their dead under kurgans. The culture was created by migrants from Eastern Europe, above all bearers of the Yamnaya culture, who assimilated the local inhabitants. Early Bronze Age people of the Dzungarian Basin (about 3000–2800 BCE) turned out to have predominantly Afanasievo ancestry.

Sources: Афанасьевская культура — Википедия; Afanasievo culture — Wikipedia; Zhang F. et al. (2021). The genomic origins of the Bronze Age Tarim Basin mummies. Nature
