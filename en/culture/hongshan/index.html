Hongshan culture

The Hongshan culture was a Neolithic culture of the West Liao river basin in northeast China. Its sites have been found across an area from Inner Mongolia to Liaoning and are dated from about 4700 to 2900 BCE, including finds in Hebei province. It is named after Hongshanhou, a site in Hongshan District, Chifeng, which the Japanese archaeologist Torii Ryūzō discovered in 1908. The site was extensively excavated in 1935 by Kōsaku Hamada and Mizuno Seiichi.
