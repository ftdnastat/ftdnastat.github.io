Lucayan people

The Lucayan people were the Indigenous inhabitants of the Bahamas and the Turks and Caicos Islands before the European colonisation of the Americas. Some scholars regard them as a branch of the Taínos, while others do not classify them as Taínos. They were the first Indigenous Americans encountered by Christopher Columbus, in October 1492. Shortly after contact the Spanish kidnapped and enslaved them, and by 1520 the Lucayans had been completely eradicated from the Bahamas. The name Lucayan is an anglicisation of the Spanish Lucayos, which derives from their own name Lukku-Cairi, meaning people of the islands.

Sources: Лукаяны — Википедия; Lucayan people — Wikipedia
