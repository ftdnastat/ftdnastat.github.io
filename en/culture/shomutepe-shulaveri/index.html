Shulaveri–Shomu culture

The Shulaveri–Shomu culture, also known as the Shulaveri-Shomutepe-Aratashen culture, is an archaeological culture of the Late Neolithic and Eneolithic. It existed on the territory of present-day Georgia, Azerbaijan and Armenia, as well as parts of northern Iran. It lasted from around the end of the 7th millennium BCE to the beginning of the 5th millennium BCE. It is named after the settlements of Shulaveri-gora in Georgia and Shomutepe in Azerbaijan.

Sources: Шулавери-шомутепинская культура — Википедия; Shulaveri–Shomu culture — Wikipedia
