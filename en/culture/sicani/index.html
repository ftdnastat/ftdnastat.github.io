Sicani

The Sicani were the earliest population of Sicily known from ancient sources and are presumed to be pre-Indo-European. They were one of three ancient peoples of the island present at the time of Phoenician and Greek colonization. They lived east of the Elymians and west of the Sicels; according to Diodorus Siculus, after a series of battles the boundary with the Sicels ran along the ancient Himera river, today the Salso. In the 13th century BCE they were pushed back by the Sicels and Elymians, and later they were Hellenized. The Castelluccio archaeological culture corresponds to the Sicani.

Sources: Сиканы — Википедия; Sicani — Wikipedia
