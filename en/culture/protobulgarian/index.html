Bulgars

The Bulgars were Turkic semi-nomadic warrior tribes that flourished in the Pontic–Caspian steppe and the Volga region between the 5th and 7th centuries. They founded Old Great Bulgaria around 630–635, which the Khazars defeated in 668. In 681 Khan Asparukh established Danubian Bulgaria, the First Bulgarian Empire, where the Bulgars were a political and military elite before merging with the Byzantine and Slavic population. Most of the remaining Pontic Bulgars moved to the Volga in the 7th century and founded Volga Bulgaria, keeping their identity into the 13th century. They preserved steppe military titles and customs, pagan shamanism and belief in the sky deity Tangra.
