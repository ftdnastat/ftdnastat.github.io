Pazyryk culture

The Pazyryk culture was a nomadic Saka Iron Age archaeological culture of the 6th to 3rd centuries BCE, identified by artefacts and mummified humans found in the Siberian permafrost of the Altai Mountains, Kazakhstan and Mongolia. The mummies lie in long barrows, or kurgans, similar to the tomb mounds of Scythian culture in Ukraine. The type site is the Pazyryk burials on the Ukok Plateau, where the Siberian Ice Princess was also found, and the finds suggest a flourishing culture that benefited from trade routes and passing caravans. The Pazyryk are considered to have led a war-like life. The culture was preceded by the Arzhan culture of the 8th–7th centuries BCE.

Sources: Пазырыкская культура — Википедия; Pazyryk culture — Wikipedia
