Baiuvarii

The Baiuvarii were an ancient Germanic tribe; they are first mentioned in the early 6th century, when they were defeated by the Franks. At first they lived south of the Danube, in the former Roman province of Raetia, in the south of present-day Bavaria. Later a duchy of their own took shape under the Agilolfing dynasty, and in the 7th–8th centuries it broke away from Merovingian rule. The genomes of 41 individuals from Bavaria of the turn of the 5th and 6th centuries showed that the men were close in ancestry to present-day northern and central Europeans, while the women were very heterogeneous; women with artificially deformed skulls probably came from southeastern Europe.

Sources: Бавары — Википедия; Baiuvarii — Wikipedia; Veeramah K. R. et al. (2018). Population genomic analysis of elongated skulls reveals extensive female-biased immigration in Early Medieval Bavaria. PNAS
