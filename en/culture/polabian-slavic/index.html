Polabian Slavs

The Polabian Slavs were West Slavic tribes who lived along the Elbe in what is today eastern Germany. Their lands extended from the Baltic Sea to the river Saale and the Ore Mountains. Three confederations are distinguished: the Obotrites in the north, the Veleti or Liutizi in the center and east, and the Lusatian Sorbs in the south. Most were peasants engaged in agriculture and animal husbandry, while some worked as fishermen, beekeepers or trappers. By the 12th–14th centuries most had been Germanized; the Sorbs of Lusatia retained their identity. Ancient DNA shows that in eastern Germany the change of ancestry in the 6th–8th centuries coincided with intensified genetic relatedness and patrilocality.

Sources: Полабские славяне — Википедия; Polabian Slavs — Wikipedia; Gretzinger J. et al. (2025). Ancient DNA connects large-scale migration with the spread of Slavs. Nature
