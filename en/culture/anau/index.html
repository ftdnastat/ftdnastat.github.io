Anau culture

The Anau culture was an ancient agricultural civilization of Central Asia centred in southern Turkmenistan. It started during the Chalcolithic period around 4000 BC, following the Neolithic Jeitun culture, and is named after its main site of Anau. The Namazga culture was contemporary with it. Pottery similar to that of the earliest Anau IA phase has been found as far away as Shir Ashian Tepe in the Semnan Province of Iran.

Sources: Анауская культура — Википедия; Anau culture — Wikipedia
