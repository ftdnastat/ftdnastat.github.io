Glazkovo culture

The Glazkovo culture was left by hunters and fishers of the East Siberian taiga in the Bronze Age (18th–13th centuries BCE); its sites are known in the Baikal region, along the Angara, on the upper Lena and the lower Selenga. It goes back to the earlier Serovo culture and is named after a necropolis in the Glazkovo suburb of Irkutsk, where the first burials were found. Its people sewed boats of birch bark and made birch-bark and wooden vessels, composite bows, copper knives and bronze fishhooks.

Sources: Глазковская культура — Википедия; Glazkov culture — Wikipedia
