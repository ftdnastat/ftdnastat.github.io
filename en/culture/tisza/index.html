Tisza culture

The Tisza culture is an archaeological culture of the Alföld plain in Central Europe, covering modern-day Hungary, western Romania, eastern Slovakia and the Ukrainian Zakarpattia Oblast. It is dated to between 5400 BCE and 4500/4400 BCE and is classed as Neolithic or, in the Russian-language literature, as early Chalcolithic. It existed in the middle and upper basins of the Tisza River and spread into Romania, in Crișana and Banat.
