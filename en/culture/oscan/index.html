Osci

The Osci, also called Oscans or Opici, were an Italic people of Campania and Latium adiectum before and during Roman times. Their traditions belong to the legendary period of Italian history, and no consensus exists about their location and language. By the end of that period the Oscan language was spoken by a number of sovereign tribal states, the most important of which, in military prowess and wealth, were the Samnites. The Samnites rivalled Rome for about 50 years in the second half of the 4th century BCE, until they were subdued with considerable difficulty. After the wars the Oscans assimilated quickly to Roman culture, and their legacy survived only in place names and literary references.

Sources: Оски — Википедия; Osci — Wikipedia
