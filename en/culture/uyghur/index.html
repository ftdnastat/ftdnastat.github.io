Uyghur Khaganate

The Uyghur Khaganate, which called itself the Toquz Oghuz, or Nine Tribes, and was known to the Tang Chinese as the Jiuxing, was a Turkic empire that existed for about a century between the mid-8th and 9th centuries. It was a tribal confederation under the Uyghur nobility and succeeded the Eastern Turkic Khaganate. The state was feudal, with the first signs of a settled life among the Uyghurs: livestock herding, farming and crafts were developed, and towns and fortresses were built. As in earlier Turkic khaganates, the Orkhon valley, with the cities of Khara-Balgas and Beshbalik, served as the capital. After its fall some Uyghurs fled to Eastern Turkestan.

Sources: Уйгурский каганат — Википедия; Uyghur Khaganate — Wikipedia
