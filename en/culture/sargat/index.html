Sargat culture

The Sargat culture was a sedentary archaeological culture of the forest-steppes of Western Siberia, existing from the 7th century BCE to the 5th century CE. It was closely related to the neighbouring Gorokhovo culture to the west, which it seems to have gradually assimilated. The Sargat tribes used heavy Sarmatian-style armour and protected their settlements with earthen ramparts and timber walls, sometimes with watchtowers. Their kurgans are recorded from the eastern foothills of the Urals to the Baraba forest-steppe. Archaeological and genetic evidence suggests a zone of intermixture between native Ugrian or Siberian populations and steppe peoples from the south. The culture may be the polity called Northern Wuyibie in Chinese sources.

Sources: Саргатская культура — Википедия; Sargat culture — Wikipedia
