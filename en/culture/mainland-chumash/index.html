Chumash people

The Chumash are a Native American people of the central and southern coastal regions of California, extending from Morro Bay in the north to Malibu in the south and to Mount Pinos in the east. Their territory includes three of the Channel Islands: Santa Cruz, Santa Rosa and San Miguel, while the smaller Anacapa was likely inhabited seasonally because it lacked a consistent water source. Modern place names of Chumash origin include Malibu, Nipomo, Lompoc, Ojai, Pismo Beach and Simi Valley. Archaeological research shows that the Chumash have deep roots in the Santa Barbara Channel area and have lived along the southern California coast for millennia.

Sources: Чумаши — Википедия; Chumash people — Wikipedia
