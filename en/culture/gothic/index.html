Goths

The Goths were an ancient Germanic union of tribes of the East Germanic subgroup who spoke the Gothic language. In the first centuries CE they set out from the Scandinavian Peninsula and gradually settled in the northern Black Sea region and along the Danube. In 378 Goth-led forces inflicted a devastating defeat on the Romans at Adrianople, and Goths under Alaric I sacked Rome in 410. Later the Visigothic Kingdom in Spain and the Ostrogothic Kingdom in Italy arose. Mitochondrial DNA of 27 people from a 2nd–4th century cemetery at Masłomęcz in eastern Poland, attributed to the Goths, revealed a connection with Pontic-Caspian steppe groups.

Sources: Готы — Википедия; Goths — Wikipedia; Stolarek I. et al. (2019). Goth migration induced changes in the matrilineal genetic structure of the central-east European population. Scientific Reports
