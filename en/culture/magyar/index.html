Hungarians

Hungarians, also known as Magyars, are an ethnic group native to present-day Hungary and the Carpathian Basin who share a common culture, language, history and ancestry. Their language belongs to the Ugric branch of the Uralic family, alongside Khanty and Mansi. There are an estimated 14.5 million ethnic Hungarians and their descendants worldwide, about 9.6 million of them in Hungary. About 2 million live in areas that belonged to the Kingdom of Hungary before the Treaty of Trianon in 1920 and now lie in seven neighbouring countries. A diaspora lives mostly in the United States, Canada, Germany, France and the United Kingdom, and includes subgroups such as the Székelys, Csángós, Palóc and Matyó.
