Saltovo-Mayaki

Saltovo-Mayaki, also known as Saltiv, is the name archaeologists give to the early medieval culture of the Pontic steppe between the Don and the Dnieper, which flourished roughly between 700 and 950, in the time of the Khazar Khaganate. Its type sites are Mayatskoye, also called Mayaki, near the Don and Verkhnii Saltiv by the Donets, where excavations began in 1900 and 1906 respectively. In the narrow sense it is the culture of the forest-steppe Don region, formed after Alan tribes settled there. In the broad sense it is the state culture of the Khazar Khaganate, divided into an Alan forest-steppe variant and a steppe variant conventionally called Bulgar.

Sources: Салтово-маяцкая культура — Википедия; Saltovo-Mayaki — Wikipedia
