Thuringii

The Thuringii were an ancient Germanic tribe that lived on the upper Elbe, in what is now Thuringia, and led a mostly settled life. Written sources know them from the 5th century. They founded a kingdom of their own, then came under the Huns, and regained independence when the Hunnic realm fell apart after Attila's death. The kingdom included the area between the Elbe and the Saale; in the early 6th century the lands of the Thuringii stretched from the Elbe in the east to the upper Main and the Danube in the south. In 531 the Thuringii lost a war to the Franks, and their lands were divided between the Franks and the Saxons.

Sources: Тюринги — Википедия; Thuringi — Wikipedia; Gretzinger J. et al. (2025). Ancient DNA connects large-scale migration with the spread of Slavs. Nature
