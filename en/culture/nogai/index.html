Nogais

The Nogais are a Turkic people who speak Nogai, a language of the Kipchak branch of the Turkic languages. They live in Eastern Europe, including the North Caucasus and the Volga region, in Central Asia and in Turkey, mostly in northern Dagestan, Stavropol Krai, Karachay-Cherkessia, Chechnya and Astrakhan Oblast. Some also live in Dobruja, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Ukraine, and a small diaspora is found in Syria and Jordan. They descend from various Mongolic and Turkic tribes that formed the Nogai Horde. The 2021 census counted 109,000 Nogais in Russia. There are nine main Nogai groups, among them the Ak Nogai, Karagash, Koban-Nogai and Yurt-Nogai.
