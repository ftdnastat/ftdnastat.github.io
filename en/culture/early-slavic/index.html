Slavs, early period

The early Slavs were an early medieval people known from Greek and Latin sources from the 6th century as the Sclaveni. They lived in the forest and forest-steppe zones of Central and Eastern Europe from the Oder to the Middle Dnieper. In the 6th–8th centuries Slavic colonization also reached the Eastern Alps. They lived in small unfortified settlements of sunken-floor huts with stoves and engaged in hunting, farming, herding and beekeeping. Cremation predominated in burial. Ancient DNA shows that the Slavic expansion of the 6th–8th centuries involved a large-scale movement from Eastern Europe that replaced more than 80% of the local gene pool in eastern Germany, Poland and Croatia.

Sources: Славяне — Википедия; Early Slavs — Wikipedia; Gretzinger J. et al. (2025). Ancient DNA connects large-scale migration with the spread of Slavs. Nature
