Las Cogotas

The Cogotas culture is named after Las Cogotas, an archaeological site in the municipality of Cardenosa, province of Avila, Spain, which was researched by the Galician archaeologist Juan Cabré in the 1920s. The site gave its name to two different cultures: Cogotas I, a pre-Celtic culture of the Late Bronze Age, and Cogotas II, most probably Celtic, of the Iron Age. The latter is known from the upper layer of Las Cogotas, a classical settlement of the Vettones. This people inhabited the modern provinces of Avila and Salamanca, as well as parts of Toledo, Zamora, Cáceres and Tras-os-Montes in Portugal.

Sources: Коготас — Википедия; Las Cogotas — Wikipedia
