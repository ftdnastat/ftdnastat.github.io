Knovíz culture

The Knovíz culture was a Bronze Age culture of central Bohemia, dated to 1300–1050 BCE and named after the type site of Knovíz near Prague. It is described as an upper Danubian subgroup of the Late Bronze Age Urnfield culture, found mainly in Bohemia, Thuringia and Bavaria. Its settlements stood on hills and other defensible spots, ringed by palisades, with residential areas of tower-houses and farming areas of pits and granaries. The culture was similar to the neighbouring Milavce culture, but its funerary rites included occasional skeletal burials alongside cremations. Its smiths were skilled, and a distinctive type of horse is associated with it.

Sources: Кновизская культура — Википедия; Knovíz culture — Wikipedia
