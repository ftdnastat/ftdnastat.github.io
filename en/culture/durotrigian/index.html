Durotriges

The Durotriges were a Celtic tribe of ancient Britain who occupied present-day Dorset, southern Somerset and Wiltshire, and eastern Devon. They lived by farming around large hillforts and minted coins of their own even before the Romans came. After the Roman conquest their main settlements were Durnovaria (now Dorchester), the tribal capital, and Lindinis (now Ilchester); by 70 CE the tribe had been Romanized and had become part of Roman Britain. In the Late Iron Age Durotrigian women were often buried with rich grave goods. From 57 ancient genomes from their burials a large kin group linked by a single maternal line was reconstructed; the people unrelated to it, apparently incomers, were mostly men.

Sources: Дуротриги — Википедия; Durotriges — Wikipedia; Cassidy L. M. et al. (2025). Continental influx and pervasive matrilocality in Iron Age Britain. Nature
