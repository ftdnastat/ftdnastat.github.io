Horgen culture

The Horgen culture was a Neolithic culture in the west of present-day Switzerland and in the German state of Baden-Württemberg; it is dated to about 3500–2800 BCE. Its people lived in pile dwellings on lakes. It is named after excavations at Horgen-Scheller on Lake Zurich; other sites include Sipplingen on Lake Constance and Bad Buchau on the Federsee. Horgen pottery is coarse, thick-walled and cylindrical. The 96 genomes from Switzerland, southern Germany and Alsace show that ancestry related to Late Neolithic pastoralists of the Pontic–Caspian steppe had appeared in Switzerland as early as 2860–2460 BCE.

Sources: Хоргенская культура — Википедия; Horgen culture — Wikipedia; Furtwängler A. et al. (2020). Ancient genomes reveal social and genetic structure of Late Neolithic Switzerland. Nature Communications
