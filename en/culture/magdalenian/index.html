Magdalenian

The Magdalenian is a technocomplex of the Late Upper Paleolithic in parts of Western and Central Europe, dating from around 21,000 to 13,000 years before present. It is named after the type site of Abri de la Madeleine, a rock shelter in the Vézère valley in Dordogne, France. Magdalenian peoples are associated with reindeer hunting, and their sites also show hunting of red deer, wild horses and other megafauna of the late glacial period. Sites stretched from Portugal in the west to Poland in the east. They produced a wide variety of art, including figurines and cave paintings, as at Lascaux and Altamira. Genetic studies indicate descent mainly from earlier Western European groups such as the Gravettians.
