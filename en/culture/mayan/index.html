Maya civilization

The Maya were a Mesoamerican civilization known for its writing, art and architecture. Their territory comprised south-eastern Mexico, all of Guatemala and Belize, and the western parts of Honduras and El Salvador. The Preclassic period is dated to 2000 BCE – 250 CE and the Classic period to 250–900 CE. Major cities included Tikal, Palenque, Chichén Itzá and Copán. Maize was the dietary staple, and the script was logosyllabic. In the 9th century the cities of the central region collapsed and were abandoned. Genomes from Copán show genetic affinity with the Late Archaic population of Belize, the Maya of Chichén Itzá and present-day Maya.

Sources: Майя (цивилизация) — Википедия; Maya civilization — Wikipedia; Murray M. et al. (2025). Ancient genomes reveal demographic trajectories during the Classic Maya period. Current Biology
