La Tène culture

The La Tène culture was a Celtic archaeological culture of the Iron Age, dated roughly to the 5th–1st centuries BCE. It developed out of the Hallstatt tradition without a clear break and spread across Central and Western Europe. In the 2nd century BCE fortified oppida appeared, some of them, such as Bibracte, becoming centers of trade and craft. Weapons were made of iron, and the art is characterized by ornament with plant, animal and geometric motifs. Burials are mostly flat graves; chariot burials occur in the early and middle phases. A study of Iron Age DNA from France found no discontinuity with the Bronze Age.

Sources: Латенская культура — Википедия; La Tène culture — Wikipedia; Fischer C.-E. et al. (2022). Origin and mobility of Iron Age Gaulish groups in present-day France revealed through archaeogenomics. iScience
