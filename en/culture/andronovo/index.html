Andronovo culture

The Andronovo culture is a collection of similar local Late Bronze Age cultures that flourished around 2000–1150 BCE, spanning from the southern Urals to the upper Yenisei in central Siberia and western Xinjiang, with southern sites reaching Tajikistan and Uzbekistan. Scholars agree that it was Indo-Iranian, though some prefer to call it an archaeological complex or horizon. Its first stage may have begun in the late 3rd millennium BCE, with a focus on cattle grazing in the vast grasslands. The slightly older Sintashta culture, once included within Andronovo, is now thought to be distinct from the early Andronovo cultures.

Sources: Андроновская культура — Википедия; Andronovo culture — Wikipedia
