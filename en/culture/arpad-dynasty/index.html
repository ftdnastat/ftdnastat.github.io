Árpád dynasty

The Árpád dynasty was the ruling house of the Principality of Hungary in the 9th and 10th centuries and of the Kingdom of Hungary from 1000 to 1301. It was named after Grand Prince Árpád, head of the Hungarian tribal federation during the conquest of the Carpathian Basin around 895, and was earlier called the Turul dynasty or kindred. Both the first Grand Prince, Álmos, and the first king, Saint Stephen, belonged to it. The dynasty adopted Christianity as the state religion, and eight of its members were canonized or beatified by the Catholic Church, so since the 13th century it has often been called the Kindred of the Holy Kings. The line ended in 1301 with the death of Andrew III.

Sources: Арпады — Википедия; Árpád dynasty — Wikipedia
