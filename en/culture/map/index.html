Ancient samples on the map

Move the slider: the map shows where samples of that time were found. The colour is the culture or the haplogroup letter. A patch shows where samples were dug up, not where a culture ended.

Y samples come from FamilyTreeDNA Discover, mt ones from Mitotree. We skip points outside the sample's country. Samples with only an era-and-region label stay grey. A group of cultures gets one colour. All archaeological cultures
