Mazunino

The Mazunino culture was an archaeological culture of the 3rd to 5th centuries CE in the middle course of the Kama River and the lower and middle course of the Belaya River. It is named after the Mazunino burial ground and was identified in the mid-1950s by V. F. Gening on the basis of excavations of cemeteries in southern Udmurtia. It was the successor of the local Pyany Bor culture. Its sites fall into two groups, the Kama and the Belaya, and in the 6th–7th centuries the Bakhmutino culture took shape on the basis of the Belaya variant.
