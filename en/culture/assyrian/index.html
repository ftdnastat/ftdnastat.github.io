Assyria

Assyria was a major ancient Mesopotamian civilisation that existed from the 21st to the 7th century BCE and conquered and administered most of West Asia from its heartland in Upper Mesopotamia. Its first capital, Assur, was founded around 2600 BCE. Assyrian history is usually divided into the Early, Old, Middle and Neo-Assyrian periods and a post-imperial one. It reached its peak in the Neo-Assyrian period, when it ruled the largest empire then assembled, from parts of modern Iran to Egypt. The empire fell in the late 7th century BCE to a coalition of Babylonians, Medes and Scythians, yet the Assyrian people survive to this day.
