Dian kingdom

Dian was an ancient kingdom of the Dian people, a metalworking civilization that was not Han and lived around the Dian Lake plateau in central northern Yunnan from the late Spring and Autumn period until the Eastern Han dynasty. Its culture goes back to at least the 8th century BCE. The Dian buried their dead in vertical pit graves, and their language was probably Tibeto-Burman. In 1954 Chinese archaeologists opened the tombs of Dian rulers at Shizhaishan, where drum finials and other bronzes showed a very high standard of casting. The Han annexed the kingdom in 109 BCE, which led to the founding of the Yizhou commandery.
