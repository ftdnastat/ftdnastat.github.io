Pitted Ware culture

The Pitted Ware culture (c. 3500–2300 BCE) was a hunter-gatherer culture of southern Scandinavia, found mainly along the coasts of Svealand, Götaland, Åland, northeastern Denmark and southern Norway. Despite its Mesolithic economy it is classed as Neolithic, because it falls within the period when farming reached Scandinavia. Its people were largely maritime hunters and traded actively with Scandinavian farmers and other Baltic hunter-gatherers. The culture emerged in east-central Sweden and gradually replaced the Funnelbeaker culture on the coasts. It was absorbed by the Battle Axe culture by 2300 BCE, and the Nordic Bronze Age combined elements of both.

Sources: Культура ямочной керамики — Википедия; Pitted Ware culture — Wikipedia
