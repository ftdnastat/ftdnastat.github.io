Maeotians

The Maeotians were tribes that in the 1st millennium BCE lived along the eastern and southeastern shores of the Sea of Azov; ancient authors called the sea itself Maeotis. They were settled farmers and fishers: their open settlements and earthen structures are known on the Taman Peninsula and along the middle Kuban. In the 4th–3rd centuries BCE many Maeotian communities became part of the Bosporan Kingdom. Which linguistic and ethnic group the Maeotians belonged to remains disputed. The mitochondrial DNA of Maeotians of the 1st–3rd centuries CE showed that they were genetically more diverse than the Sarmatians and that the two groups mixed intensively.

Sources: Меоты — Википедия; Maeotians — Wikipedia; Morozova I. Yu. et al. (2013). Some features of mitochondrial gene pool of Maeotis in light of their relation to Cis-Asov nomads. Genetika
