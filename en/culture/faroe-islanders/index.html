Faroe Islanders

The Faroese, or Faroe Islanders, are an ethnic group native to the Faroe Islands and are of mixed Norse and British origins. About 21,000 Faroese live in neighbouring countries, particularly Denmark, Iceland and Norway. Most are citizens of the Kingdom of Denmark, in which the Faroe Islands are a constituent nation. Their language, Faroese, is a North Germanic tongue closely related to Icelandic and to western Norwegian varieties; many also know Danish and Norwegian, and young people English. Most Faroese belong to the Faroese People's Church, which is a member of the Lutheran World Federation.
