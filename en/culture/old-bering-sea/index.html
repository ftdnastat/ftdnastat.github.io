Old Bering Sea

Old Bering Sea is an archaeological culture centred on the Bering Strait region and marked by an elaborate circle-and-dot art style. No known site lies more than 1 km from the ocean, and despite the name several sites are on the Chukchi Sea. Its time range is given as 400 BCE to possibly as late as 1300 CE, while another suggested range is about 200 BCE to 500 CE. Following Henry B. Collins, it is regarded as the initial phase of the Northern Maritime tradition. Russian scholarship calls it Paleo-Eskimo or Eskimo proper, a point that remains debated, and places it between the 3rd century BCE and the 7th–8th centuries CE.

Sources: Древнеберингоморская культура — Википедия; Old Bering Sea — Wikipedia
