Lola culture

The Lola culture was a Middle Bronze Age culture which flourished in the North Caucasus and the Volga-Ural steppes from about 2200 BC to 1800 BC. It is named after the settlement of Lola, near Elista.

Sources: Лолинская культура — Википедия; Lola culture — Wikipedia
