Inca Empire

The Inca Empire, or Tawantinsuyu, was an Indigenous state of South America in the 11th–16th centuries, the largest on the continent both in area and in population. Its lands stretched from present-day Pasto in Colombia to the Maule River in Chile. The state is known for monumental architecture, stonework above all, for the extensive Qhapaq Ñan road network and for the knotted quipu cords used for record keeping. In 1533 most of the empire came under the control of Spanish conquistadors, and in 1572 the Inca state ceased to exist. A paleogenomic study of 89 individuals from the Andes showed that people of different ancestries lived side by side in the heartlands of the Tiwanaku and Inca states.

Sources: Империя инков — Википедия; Inca Empire — Wikipedia; Nakatsuka N. et al. (2020). A paleogenomic reconstruction of the deep population history of the Andes. Cell
