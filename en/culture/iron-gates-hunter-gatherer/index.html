Iron Gates Mesolithic

The Iron Gates Mesolithic is a Mesolithic culture of the Iron Gates of the Danube in Romania and Serbia, dated to 13,000–6,000 cal BCE. Its people lived as hunter-gatherers, taking food from the land and the Danube. Lepenski Vir, a site of Mesolithic hunters in Serbia, consists of one large settlement with ten small satellite settlements. A reinterpretation of Lepenski Vir based on ancient genomes found that house construction, village life and farming there were associated primarily with Europe's first farmers, while Iron Gates foragers and their admixed descendants were largely buried separately, on the fringes of the settlement.

Sources: Лепенски-Вир — Википедия; Iron Gates Mesolithic — Wikipedia; Brami M. et al. (2022). Was the Fishing Village of Lepenski Vir Built by Europe's First Farmers? Journal of World Prehistory
