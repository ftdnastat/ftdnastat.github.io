Starčevo culture

The Starčevo culture is a Neolithic archaeological culture of Southeastern Europe, dated to c. 6200–4500 BCE. It arose from the spread of the Neolithic package of farming and ceramics from Asia Minor to the area of Sesklo, and marks the arrival of that package in the inland Balkans, as the Cardial ware culture did along the Adriatic coast. It forms part of the wider Starčevo–Criş culture, which gave rise to the Linear Pottery culture of central Europe about 700 years after Neolithic farmers first spread towards the northern Balkans. The type site lies on the north bank of the Danube near Starčevo in Vojvodina, Serbia, opposite Belgrade.

Sources: Старчево-кришская культура — Википедия; Starčevo culture — Wikipedia
