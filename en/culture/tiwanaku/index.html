Tiwanaku

Tiwanaku is a Pre-Columbian archaeological site in western Bolivia near Lake Titicaca, about 70 kilometers from La Paz, and one of the largest sites in South America. Surface remains cover around 4 square kilometers and include decorated ceramics, monumental structures and megalithic blocks. It has been conservatively estimated that 10,000 to 20,000 people lived there in AD 800. The site was first recorded in writing in 1549 by the Spanish conquistador Pedro Cieza de León. The Jesuit chronicler Bernabé Cobo reported that its name was once taypiqala, Aymara for stone in the center, and some scholars suggest that Puquina was most likely the language of its inhabitants, who had no writing.
