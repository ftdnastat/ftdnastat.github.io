mtDNA haplogroup L2

L2 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 1,354 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Jamaica (38%), Sudan (25%), Dominican Republic (16%). The branch's common foremother lived about 87k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 79k to 95k years ago. YFull has 2,155 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
