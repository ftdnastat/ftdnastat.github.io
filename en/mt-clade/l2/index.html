mtDNA haplogroup L2

L2 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 1,347 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Jamaica (38% of kits), Sudan (25% of kits), Dominican Republic (15% of kits). The branch's common foremother lived about 87k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 79k to 95k years ago. YFull has 2,153 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
