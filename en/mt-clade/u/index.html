mtDNA haplogroup U

Path from the root:L3 67k years›N 59k years›R 58k years›U

U is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 29,901 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Finland (29% of kits), Belarus (31% of kits), Lithuania (30% of kits). The branch's common foremother lived about 51k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 48k to 55k years ago. YFull has 16,408 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
