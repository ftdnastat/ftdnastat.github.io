mtDNA haplogroup U

Path from the root:L3 66k years›N 59k years›R 58k years›U

U is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 29,909 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Finland (29%), Belarus (31%), Lithuania (30%). The branch's common foremother lived about 51k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 47k to 55k years ago. YFull has 16,408 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.

How the branch grew #

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 675.

The branch grew fastest 11k–1,000 years ago: its sub-branches went from 132 to 673.

Sub-branches of the last centuries show up only where many relatives have tested, so the right end of the curve says more about who took a test than about population growth.
