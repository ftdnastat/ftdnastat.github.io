Migration path #

How the branch's maternal line moved. Up to branch M, the path follows the Mitotree maps in FamilyTreeDNA Discover. After that, dotted, comes ftdnastat's estimate. It places each ancestor by the countries where the branch's FamilyTreeDNA customers live and routes the way between them over the terrain. Checked on Y branches: for half of the nodes younger than 6,000 years the estimate lies within 70–200 km of Discover's point, for older nodes, within about 550 km. Nodes older than 30,000 years were not checked. Dates are node ages. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.
