How the branch's maternal line moved. Up to branch M, the path follows the Mitotree maps in FamilyTreeDNA Discover. After that, dotted, comes ftdnastat's estimate. Checked on Y branches: for half of the nodes younger than 6,000 years the estimate lies within 70–200 km of Discover's point, for older nodes, within about 550 km.
