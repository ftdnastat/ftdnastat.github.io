G is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 111 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: South Korea (9.8%), Japan (7.4%), Kazakhstan (6.0%). The branch's common foremother lived about 34k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 30k to 38k years ago. YFull has 878 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.

How the branch grew #

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 67.
