The branch's common foremother lived about 27k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 25k to 30k years ago. YFull has 2,788 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.

How the branch grew #

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 135.

The branch grew fastest 16k–11k years ago: its sub-branches went from 15 to 33.

Sub-branches of the last centuries show up only where many relatives have tested, so the right end of the curve says more about who took a test than about population growth.
