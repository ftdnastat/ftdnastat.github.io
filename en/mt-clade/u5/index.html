mtDNA haplogroup U5

Path from the root:L3 66k years›N 59k years›R 58k years›U 51k years›U5

U5 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 11,369 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Finland (19%); Norway (12%); Chuvashia, Russia (16%). The branch's common foremother lived about 43k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 41k to 46k years ago. YFull has 5,350 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
