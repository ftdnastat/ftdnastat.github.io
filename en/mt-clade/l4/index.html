mtDNA haplogroup L4

L4 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 77 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ethiopia (9.9%); Saudi Arabia (1.7%); Native American, United States (0.2%). The branch's common foremother lived about 74k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 68k to 81k years ago. YFull has 163 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
