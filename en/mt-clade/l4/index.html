mtDNA haplogroup L4

L4 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 77 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Ethiopia (10% of kits), Saudi Arabia (1.7% of kits), United States (Native American) (0.2% of kits). The branch's common foremother lived about 74k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 68k to 81k years ago. YFull has 162 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
