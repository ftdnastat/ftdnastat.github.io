mtDNA haplogroup L1

L1 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 734 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Jamaica (21%), Brazil (8.2%), Dominican Republic (9.6%). The branch's common foremother lived about 111k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 102k to 120k years ago. YFull has 1,303 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
