mtDNA haplogroup L1

L1 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 734 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Jamaica (21% of kits), Brazil (8.2% of kits), Dominican Republic (9.6% of kits). The branch's common foremother lived about 110k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 103k to 118k years ago. YFull has 1,303 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
