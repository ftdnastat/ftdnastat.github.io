L3e is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 722 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Dominican Republic (14%), Jamaica (11%), Cuba (7.6%). The branch's common foremother lived about 36k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 33k to 40k years ago. YFull has 1,273 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
