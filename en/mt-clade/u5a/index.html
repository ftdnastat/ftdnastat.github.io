mtDNA haplogroup U5a

Path from the root:L3 66k years›N 59k years›R 58k years›U 51k years›U5 43k years›U5a

U5a is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 6,063 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Chuvashia, Russia (14%); Estonia (11%); Chechnya, Russia (9.9%). The branch's common foremother lived about 21k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 19k to 23k years ago. YFull has 2,853 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 16k years ago.

Migration path #

How the branch's maternal line moved. Up to branch U5, the path follows the Mitotree maps in FamilyTreeDNA Discover. After that, dotted, comes ftdnastat's estimate. It places each ancestor by the countries where the branch's FamilyTreeDNA customers live and routes the way between them over the terrain. Checked on Y branches: for half of the nodes younger than 6,000 years the estimate lies within 70–200 km of Discover's point, for older nodes, within about 550 km. Nodes older than 30,000 years were not checked. Dates are node ages. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.

How the branch grew #

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 100.

The branch grew fastest 6,000–3,500 years ago: its sub-branches went from 30 to 66.

Sub-branches of the last centuries show up only where many relatives have tested, so the right end of the curve says more about who took a test than about population growth.
