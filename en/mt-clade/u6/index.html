mtDNA haplogroup U6

Path from the root:L3 66k years›N 59k years›R 58k years›U 51k years›U6

U6 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 452 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Morocco (7.1%), Spain (2.5%), Algeria (4.2%). The branch's common foremother lived about 41k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 37k to 46k years ago. YFull has 743 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
