I is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 3,507 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of Chechnya) (8.9% of kits), Scotland (4.8% of kits), Iceland (8.8% of kits). The branch's common foremother lived about 17k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 16k to 18k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N1a1b, split off about 28k years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,523 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
