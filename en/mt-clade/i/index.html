How the branch grew #

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 58.

The branch grew fastest 5,000–3,000 years ago: its sub-branches went from 28 to 44.

Sub-branches of the last centuries show up only where many relatives have tested, so the right end of the curve says more about who took a test than about population growth.
