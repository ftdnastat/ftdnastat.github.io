The branch's common foremother lived about 29k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 26k to 34k years ago. How the branch grew #

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 187.
