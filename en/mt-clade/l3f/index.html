L3f is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 208 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Sudan (11%), Ethiopia (8.8%), Jamaica (4.7%). The branch's common foremother lived about 51k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 46k to 57k years ago.
