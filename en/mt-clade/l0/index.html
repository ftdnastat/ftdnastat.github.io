mtDNA haplogroup L0

L0 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 325 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: South Africa (29%), Ethiopia (11%), Sudan (11%). The branch's common foremother lived about 116k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 103k to 129k years ago. YFull has 1,892 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.

How many sub-branches already existed at each moment: a sub-branch counts from the time its common ancestress lived (estimates of the Mitotree in FamilyTreeDNA Discover). Sub-branches with a known age: 140.
