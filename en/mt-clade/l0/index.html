mtDNA haplogroup L0

L0 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 324 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: South Africa (29% of kits), Ethiopia (11% of kits), Sudan (11% of kits). The branch's common foremother lived about 116k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 105k to 127k years ago. YFull has 1,889 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 20k years ago.
