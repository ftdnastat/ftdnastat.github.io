mtDNA haplogroup U4

Path from the root:L3 66k years›N 59k years›R 58k years›U 51k years›U4

U4 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA mt tree with 3,137 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Montenegro (9.5%), Estonia (6.1%), Serbia (6.5%). The branch's common foremother lived about 22k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 19k to 24k years ago. YFull has 1,732 samples in this branch and dates its common foremother to 13k years ago.
