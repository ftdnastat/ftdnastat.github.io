R-Y2395 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,589 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-Z284, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Isle of Man (35%), Norway (19%), Sweden (11%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,400 years ago.
