I-CTS7676 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,917 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-L287, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Finland (16%); Karelia, Russia (8.8%); Sweden (1.7%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 2,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,200 to 2,900 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-Y2563, split off about 2,800 years ago by Discover. YFull has 278 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,300 years ago.

Belongs to the I-L22 branch, which has a note.
