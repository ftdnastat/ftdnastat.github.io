J-Z600 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,586 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-Z597, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Albania (14%); Kosovo (15%); Mordovia, Russia (7.4%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,400 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,700 to 6,200 years ago. YFull has 617 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,500 years ago.

Belongs to the J-M102 branch, which has a note.
