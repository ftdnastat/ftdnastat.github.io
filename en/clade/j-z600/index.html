J-Z600 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,577 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-Z597, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Albania (14% of kits), Kosovo (15% of kits), Macedonia (7.2% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,400 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,700 to 6,200 years ago. YFull has 618 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,500 years ago.
