Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M172 28k years›J-M410 20k years›J-L26 17k years›J-Z2227 15k years›J-Z2231 14k years›J-M67

J-M67 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,025 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ingushetia, Russia (61%); Chechnya, Russia (35%); Orstkhoy (Aosta Valley in FTDNA) (16%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 13k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 15k years ago. YFull has 1,539 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 12k years ago.

Wikipedia notes that older papers called M67 J2f. It says the highest frequencies are associated with Nakh peoples, and also gives 13.3% among Georgians, 10.2% among Cretans and 9.6% among north-central Italians.
