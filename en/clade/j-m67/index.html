J-M67 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,022 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of Ingushetia) (61% of kits), Russia (Republic of Chechnya) (35% of kits), Orstkhoy (reported as Italy · Aosta Valley) (16% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 13k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 15k years ago. YFull has 1,539 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 12k years ago.

Wikipedia notes that older papers called M67 J2f. It says the highest frequencies are associated with Nakh peoples, and also gives 13.3% among Georgians, 10.2% among Cretans and 9.6% among north-central Italians.
