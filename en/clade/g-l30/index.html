G-L30 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 6,104 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Adygea, Russia (34%); Abkhazia, Georgia (29%); Kabardino-Balkaria, Russia (18%).
