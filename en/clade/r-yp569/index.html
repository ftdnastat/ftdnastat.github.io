R-YP569 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 588 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-YP575, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Karelia, Russia (11%); Russia (3.7%); Belarus (2.6%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 2,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,200 to 2,900 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-YP672, split off about 3,200 years ago by Discover. YFull has 244 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,000 years ago.

Belongs to the R-Z280 branch, which has a note.
