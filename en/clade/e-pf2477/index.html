E-PF2477 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,185 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-CTS6185, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Morocco (26% of kits), Algeria (21% of kits), Libya (19% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 2,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,200 to 3,000 years ago. YFull has 623 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,300 years ago.
