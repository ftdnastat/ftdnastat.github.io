R-U152 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 10,223 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Switzerland (17% of kits), France (12% of kits), Italy (12% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,300 years ago. YFull has 1,484 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

Wikipedia, citing Myres et al., says U152 is most frequent (20–44%) in Switzerland, Italy, France and western Poland. It states that ancient samples from the Bell Beaker, Hallstatt and Tumulus cultures belonged to this subclade. Iron Age Latins, Etruscans and Alpine Celts, it adds, were mostly U152.
