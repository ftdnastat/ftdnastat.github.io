Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M172 28k years›J-M410 20k years›J-CTS7683 19k years›J-Z6049

J-Z6049 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 244 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (12%); Georgia (4.0%); Adygea, Russia (3.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 17k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 19k years ago.

Schurr et al. (2023) write that the Z6046 branch is found in abundance among present-day Mingrelians; in the FTDNA tree this SNP sits on the Z6049 node. According to the paper, this J2a1 branch is ancestral to the line of the Mesolithic man from the Kotias Klde rock shelter in western Georgia.

Sources: Schurr et al. 2023 ↗
