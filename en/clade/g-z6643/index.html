G-Z6643 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 357 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-Z6638, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (38%); Karachay-Cherkessia, Russia (21%); Kabardino-Balkaria, Russia (18%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,800 to 6,400 years ago. YFull has 232 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,200 years ago.

Belongs to the G-P15 branch, which has a note.
