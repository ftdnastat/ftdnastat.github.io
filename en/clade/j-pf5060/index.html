J-PF5060 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 272 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-PF5015, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Tajikistan (2.9%); Lebanon (1.9%); Mordovia, Russia (2.6%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 9,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 8,600 to 12k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, J-PF5040, split off about 11k years ago by Discover.

Belongs to the J-M410 branch, which has a note.
