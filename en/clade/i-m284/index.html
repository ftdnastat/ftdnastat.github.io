I-M284 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,570 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-L1195, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Northern Ireland (5.1%), Ireland (2.1%), Scotland (2.0%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 9,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 8,500 to 12k years ago. YFull has 352 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 7,100 years ago.

Belongs to the I-M223 branch, which has a note.
