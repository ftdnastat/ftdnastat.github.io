T-CTS7703 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,035 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, T-CTS8512, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: United Arab Emirates (16%), Bahrain (7.7%), Palestine (4.6%).
