Haplogroup E-V13

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-M35 25k years›E-M78 15k years›E-Z1919 13k years›E-V13

Equivalent branches, the same kits: E-L618 (5,042 kits, 9,300 years), E-CTS1975 (5,026 kits, 8,900 years), E-PF2252 (5,026 kits, 8,900 years)

E-V13 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,042 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Kosovo (36%), Albania (28%), Montenegro (20%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,500 to 6,000 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-L618, split off about 9,300 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,381 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,800 years ago.

Wikipedia says E-V13 dominates the European E-V68 lineages, except in Iberia, and peaks in the Balkans and Italy. Lacan et al. (2011) reported it in a man of about 7,000 years ago from Avellaner Cave, Catalonia. The article places two Lengyel-culture individuals from Hungary and Croatia in L618, the predecessor of V13.
