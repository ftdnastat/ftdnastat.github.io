Path from the root:R-M207 28k years›R-M173 22k years›R-M420 17k years›R-M198 8,500 years›R-M417 5,300 years›R-Z282 4,800 years›R-Z280

R-Z280 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 6,120 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Mordovia, Russia (26%); Slovenia (24%); Chuvashia, Russia (22%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,400 years ago. YFull has 2,080 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,600 years ago.

According to Wikipedia, Balts, East Slavs, Serbs, Macedonians, Bulgarians and Romanians carry more Z280 than M458, and its sub-branch Z92 is common among them, sometimes prevailing. It is the other way round for West Slavs and Hungarians: Z92 is rare there, and Czech and Slovenian samples lack it altogether. In Inner and Central Asia Z280 occurs alongside the Asian Z93.
