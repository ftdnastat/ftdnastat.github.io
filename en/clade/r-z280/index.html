R-Z280 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 6,102 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Slovenia (24% of kits), Russia (Republic of Mordovia) (25% of kits), Russia (Republic of Chuvashia) (22% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,400 years ago. YFull has 2,065 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,600 years ago.

According to Wikipedia, Balts, East Slavs, Serbs, Macedonians, Bulgarians and Romanians carry more Z280 than M458, and its sub-branch Z92 is common among them, sometimes prevailing. It is the other way round for West Slavs and Hungarians: Z92 is rare there, and Czech and Slovenian samples lack it altogether. In Inner and Central Asia Z280 occurs alongside the Asian Z93.
