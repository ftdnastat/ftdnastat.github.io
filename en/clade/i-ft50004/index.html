I-FT50004 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 320 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS11603, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Sweden (0.9%), Norway (0.8%), Finland (0.3%).
