Haplogroup Q-L54

Path from the root:Q-M242 30k years›Q-M346 21k years›Q-L53 17k years›Q-L54

Q-L54 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,977 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Panama (64%), Mexico (30%), Guatemala (35%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 17k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 15k to 19k years ago. YFull has 307 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 16k years ago.

The Russian Wikipedia says the Q1b1a-L54 subclade dominates two northern Siberian populations, the Kets and the Selkups. It also lists sample BER001 from the Berendeevo burial site in Yaroslavl Oblast as carrying Q1-L54. The English Wikipedia assigns Anzick-1 from Montana to Q-L54*.
