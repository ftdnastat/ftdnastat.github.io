Equivalent branches, the same kits: R-Z2103 R1b-Z2103 (3,222 kits, 6,200 years), R-M12145 (3,190 kits, 6,200 years)

R-M12149 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,222 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Armenia (20% of kits), Montenegro (17% of kits), Azerbaijan (13% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,000 to 6,500 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-Z2103, split off about 6,200 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,312 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,300 years ago.

The literature calls this branch Z2103; FamilyTreeDNA names it M12149. Wikipedia says Z2103 is prevalent in ancient DNA associated with the Yamnaya culture. It quotes Lazaridis et al. (2022): Z2103 became highly successful, briefly, as part of the Yamnaya culture and its offshoots. The Russian Wikipedia adds that the eastern branch, Z2103, moved as far as the Don and the Volga region.
