Haplogroup R-M12149 R1b-Z2103 (M12149)

Path from the root:R-M207 28k years›R-M173 22k years›R-M343 20k years›R-M269 6,500 years›R-L23 6,400 years›R-M12149

Equivalent branches, the same kits: R-Z2103 R1b-Z2103 (3,233 kits, 6,200 years), R-M12145 (3,201 kits, 6,200 years)

R-M12149 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,233 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Armenia (20%), Montenegro (17%), Azerbaijan (13%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,000 to 6,500 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-Z2103, split off about 6,200 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,312 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,300 years ago.

The literature calls this branch Z2103; FamilyTreeDNA names it M12149. Wikipedia says Z2103 is prevalent in ancient DNA associated with the Yamnaya culture. It quotes Lazaridis et al. (2022): Z2103 became highly successful, briefly, as part of the Yamnaya culture and its offshoots. The Russian Wikipedia adds that the eastern branch, Z2103, moved as far as the Don and the Volga region.

Migration path #

How the branch's ancestral line moved, as modelled by FamilyTreeDNA Discover: ancestor locations are estimated from present-day carriers, the path between them is the least-cost route over terrain. Dates are node TMRCAs, good to centuries. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.
