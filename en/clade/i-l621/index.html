I-L621 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,879 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS4002, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Bosnia and Herzegovina (31% of kits), Croatia (27% of kits), Serbia (22% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 12k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 14k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-S10302, split off about 7,400 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,152 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 6,400 years ago.
