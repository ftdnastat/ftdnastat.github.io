J-FGC10500 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 364 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Egypt (3.7% of kits), Pakistan (3.9% of kits), Kuwait (1.9% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 1,400 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 1,200 to 1,700 years ago. YFull has 137 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 1,400 years ago.

The Quraysh & Banu-Hashem project regards carriers of FGC10500 as descendants of Imam Ali. The project says it reconstructed Ali's own STR haplotype from over a hundred kits of his descendants. According to the project, Sayyids and Sharifs with well-known lines of descent tested FGC10500+ as well.
