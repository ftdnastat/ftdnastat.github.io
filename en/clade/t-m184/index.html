Haplogroup T-M184 T

T-M184 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,595 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: United Arab Emirates (20% of kits), Somalia (17% of kits), Bahrain (13% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 28k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 25k to 32k years ago.

Wikipedia calls T-M184 both geographically widespread and relatively rare, uncommon in Europe except in the south. It cites Ain Ghazal, Jordan, as the earliest presence. Egyptian and Anatolian haplotypes, it says, are much older than Omani ones: 13,700 and 9,000 years against 1,600. The Russian Wikipedia gives the highest frequencies: Fulbe 18%, Somalis 10.4%, Omanis 8.3%.
