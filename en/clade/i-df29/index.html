I-DF29 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 22,587 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Sweden (41% of kits), Norway (29% of kits), Denmark (24% of kits). YFull has 4,110 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,600 years ago.
