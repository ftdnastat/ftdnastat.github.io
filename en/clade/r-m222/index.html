R-M222 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 7,178 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ireland (17%), Northern Ireland (15%), Scotland (6.3%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 2,100 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 1,900 to 2,400 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-Z2956, split off about 3,700 years ago by Discover. YFull has 587 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,100 years ago.

The R-M222 project at FamilyTreeDNA says the branch may be 4,000 or more years old. Many carriers trace their ancestry to Ulster, north-west Ireland and Scotland. A 2005 Trinity College Dublin report linked the haplotype to the Uí Néill, the descendants of Niall of the Nine Hostages. Sequencing, the project says, has shown that the branch is much older than Niall.
