Haplogroup T-L208

Path from the root:T-M184 28k years›T-L454 13k years›T-L208

T-L208 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,044 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, T-CTS931, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: United Arab Emirates (18% of kits), Somalia (15% of kits), Bahrain (10% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 9,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 8,000 to 11k years ago. YFull has 859 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,300 years ago.

Belongs to the T-M184 branch, which has a note.
