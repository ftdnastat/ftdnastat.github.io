E-CTS4455 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,048 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-CTS567, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Sicily, Italy (8.9%); Eritrea (10%); Palestine (5.9%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 8,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 7,500 to 9,800 years ago. YFull has 494 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,200 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M78 branch, which has a note.
