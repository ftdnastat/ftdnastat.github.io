Haplogroup I-M170

Equivalent branches, the same kits: I-L758 I (47,085 kits, 35k years)

I-M170 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 47,085 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Sweden (48% of kits), Bosnia and Herzegovina (47% of kits), Denmark (42% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 28k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 24k to 31k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-L758, split off about 35k years ago by Discover. YFull has 7,740 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 28k years ago.
