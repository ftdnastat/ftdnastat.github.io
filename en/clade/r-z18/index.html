R-Z18 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,884 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Norway (4.8%); Flanders, Belgium (8.2%); Sweden (3.9%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,300 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,700 to 4,900 years ago. YFull has 378 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,800 years ago.

Wikipedia dates Z18 to about 2850 BCE and calls it a branch of U106. It says Z18 is most concentrated in Norway and Sweden, and that the earliest sample comes from the Late Neolithic on Zealand, Denmark. The article says Germanic peoples spread Z18 around Europe during the Migration Period and the Viking Age.
