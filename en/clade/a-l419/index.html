Haplogroup A-L419

A-L419 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 364 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Eritrea (12% of kits), Ethiopia (8.1% of kits), Kenya (7.2% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 121k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 106k to 139k years ago. YFull has 189 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 126k years ago.
