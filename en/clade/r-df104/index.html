R-DF104 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,855 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-DF105, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Ireland (11% of kits), Northern Ireland (11% of kits), Scotland (4.3% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 1,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 1,600 to 2,200 years ago. YFull has 479 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 1,800 years ago.

Belongs to the R-L21 branch, which has a note.
