R-Z8 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,379 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Netherlands (4.8%); England (3.5%); Flanders, Belgium (6.1%). The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-Z5, split off about 3,400 years ago by Discover. YFull has 327 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,900 years ago.
