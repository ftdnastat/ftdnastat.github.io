I-Z74 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,857 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Finland (17%), Norway (8.6%), Sweden (8.0%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,100 to 4,000 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-CTS6868, split off about 3,800 years ago by Discover. YFull has 571 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,900 years ago.

Belongs to the I-L22 branch, which has a note.

Migration path #

How the branch's ancestral line moved, as modelled by FamilyTreeDNA Discover: ancestor locations are estimated from present-day carriers, the path between them is the least-cost route over terrain. Dates are node TMRCAs, good to centuries. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.
