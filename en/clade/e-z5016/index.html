E-Z5016 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 899 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-SK888, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Albania (11% of kits), Bosnia and Herzegovina (6.8% of kits), Montenegro (6.2% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,100 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,600 to 4,800 years ago.
