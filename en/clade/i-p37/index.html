Haplogroup I-P37 I2a

Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-P215 25k years›I-L460 23k years›I-P37

I-P37 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 6,099 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Bosnia and Herzegovina (36%), Croatia (29%), Serbia (25%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 20k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 18k to 23k years ago. YFull has 1,825 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 19k years ago.

Wikipedia calls I-P37.2 I2a1a in the ISOGG-style naming. It puts the divergence of the subclade at 10.7±4.8 thousand years. The article says its branch M26 makes up about 40% of Sardinian patrilines, and L621 exceeds 45% in southern Bosnia-Herzegovina and southern Croatia.
