Haplogroup R-M417 R1a-M417

Path from the root:R-M207 28k years›R-M173 22k years›R-M420 17k years›R-M198 8,500 years›R-M417

R-M417 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 20,958 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Poland (35%), Czech Republic (33%), Slovakia (32%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,300 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,600 to 6,000 years ago. YFull has 6,113 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,300 years ago.

Wikipedia calls M417 the most widely found subclade of R1a, split into the European Z282 and the Central and South Asian Z93. It cites Underhill et al. (2014) for diversification about 3,400–5,800 years ago. The Russian Wikipedia reports that Fatyanovo-culture individuals were found to carry R1a1a1-M417.
