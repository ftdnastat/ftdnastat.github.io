G-Z6702 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 429 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-FGC715, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (38%); Georgia (16%); Karachay-Cherkessia, Russia (19%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 6,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,300 to 7,100 years ago. YFull has 295 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 6,400 years ago.
