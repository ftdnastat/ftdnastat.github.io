J-FGC8712 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 517 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Egypt (4.3% of kits), Pakistan (4.2% of kits), Saudi Arabia (2.1% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,300 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,800 to 3,800 years ago. YFull has 169 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,000 years ago.

The Quraysh & Banu-Hashem project at FamilyTreeDNA gathers carriers of FGC8712 and its sub-branches. The project says FGC8712 splits into two parallel lines: it links one to the descendants of Ishmael (the Adnanites) and the other to the descendants of Jacob. The project puts the age of the branch at 3,400–4,200 years.
