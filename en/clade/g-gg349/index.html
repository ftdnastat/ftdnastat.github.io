Equivalent branches, the same kits: G-Z3043 (294 kits, 16k years)

G-GG349 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 294 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-BY1124, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Kazakhstan (5.2%), Turkey (1.3%), Iran (1.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 17k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, G-Z3043, split off about 16k years ago by Discover. YFull has 137 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 14k years ago.

Belongs to the G-M201 branch, which has a note.
