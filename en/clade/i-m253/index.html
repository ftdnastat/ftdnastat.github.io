Haplogroup I-M253 I1

Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-M253

I-M253 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 29,793 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Sweden (45%), Norway (35%), Denmark (33%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 20k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 18k to 23k years ago.

Wikipedia says I1 peaks in Sweden, with 52% of males in Västra Götaland County. It gives national averages of about 38–39% in Sweden, 37% in Norway and 34.8% in Denmark. YFull (2022) dates the formation of I1 to 27,500 years ago and the common ancestor of present carriers to 4,600 years ago.
