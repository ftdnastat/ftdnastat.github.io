I-L849 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,475 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-S2078, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Mordovia, Russia (6.3%); Albania (5.7%); Estonia (3.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,100 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,600 to 4,800 years ago. YFull has 312 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,400 years ago.

Belongs to the I-M253 branch, which has a note.
