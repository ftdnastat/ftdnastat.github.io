I-L1286 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 596 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-L1287, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: England (0.6%), United Kingdom (0.6%), United States (0.4%). The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-S21204, split off about 16k years ago by Discover. YFull has 192 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 13k years ago.
