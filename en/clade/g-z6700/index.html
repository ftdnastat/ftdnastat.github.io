G-Z6700 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 434 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-Z6702, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of North Ossetia-Alania) (38% of kits), Georgia (16% of kits), Russia (Republic of Karachay-Cherkessia) (20% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 6,300 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,500 to 7,300 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, G-SK1108, split off about 7,900 years ago by Discover. YFull has 297 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 6,500 years ago.
