G-Z6700 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 437 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-Z6702, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (38%); Georgia (16%); Karachay-Cherkessia, Russia (19%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 6,300 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,400 to 7,300 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, G-SK1108, split off about 7,900 years ago by Discover. YFull has 299 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 6,400 years ago.

Belongs to the G-P15 branch, which has a note.
