I-Z2041 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,338 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-Z2040, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Denmark (2.4%), Sweden (1.8%), Slovenia (2.6%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,600 to 4,800 years ago. YFull has 270 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,300 years ago.

Belongs to the I-M253 branch, which has a note.
