Haplogroup J-M102 J2b

Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M172 28k years›J-M102

J-M102 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,896 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Kosovo (20% of kits), Albania (16% of kits), Oman (9.1% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 16k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago. YFull has 1,163 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 16k years ago.

Wikipedia says J2b-M102 and J2a-M410 have been present in South Asia since Neolithic times (about 9,500 years ago). It names a J2b sample from Tepe Abdul Hosein, about 8000 BC, as the earliest Neolithic appearance of J2. The Russian Wikipedia reports J2b in a Kura-Araxes-culture individual.
