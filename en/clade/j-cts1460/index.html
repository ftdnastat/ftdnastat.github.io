J-CTS1460 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 781 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Dagestan, Russia (24%); Chechnya, Russia (14%); Orstkhoy (Aosta Valley in FTDNA) (14%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,100 to 6,800 years ago. YFull has 333 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,900 years ago.

Agdzhoyan et al. (2023) write that CTS1460 is typical of speakers of the Avar-Andi-Tsez languages and date it to about 6 thousand years. The paper credits most of its weight to the sub-branch Y6916 (5.8 ± 1.1 thousand years), centred where these languages are spoken.

Sources: Agdzhoyan et al. 2023 ↗
