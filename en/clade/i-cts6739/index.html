I-CTS6739 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 915 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-F2642, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Cornish, England (2.5%); Netherlands (1.0%); Denmark (1.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,300 to 4,400 years ago. YFull has 172 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,800 years ago.
