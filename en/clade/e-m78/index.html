E-M78 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 7,516 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Somalia (60% of kits), Kosovo (36% of kits), Albania (29% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 17k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-V68, split off about 21k years ago by Discover. YFull has 2,586 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 14k years ago.
