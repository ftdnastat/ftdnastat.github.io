Haplogroup E-M78

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-M35 25k years›E-M78

Equivalent branches, the same kits: E-V68 (7,544 kits, 21k years)

E-M78 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 7,544 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Somalia (60%), Kosovo (36%), Albania (29%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 17k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-V68, split off about 21k years ago by Discover. YFull has 2,613 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 14k years ago.

Wikipedia says E-M78 is widely distributed in North Africa, the Horn of Africa and West Asia, with peak frequencies among Afroasiatic-speaking populations from Upper Egypt to Sudan. Trombetta et al. (2015) dated the mutation to about 20,300–14,800 years ago. The article adds that the men from Taforalt, Morocco (15,100–13,900 years ago), belonged to E-M78.
