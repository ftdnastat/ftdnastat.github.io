Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-M35 25k years›E-M81 4,500 years›E-Z1200

Equivalent branches, the same kits: E-FGC22807 (1,280 kits, 4,500 years), E-CTS4236 (1,276 kits, 3,800 years)

E-Z1200 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,280 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Morocco (27%), Algeria (21%), Libya (19%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,600 to 3,500 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-FGC22807, split off about 4,500 years ago by Discover. YFull has 656 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,600 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M81 branch, which has a note.

Migration path #

How the branch's ancestral line moved, as modelled by FamilyTreeDNA Discover: ancestor locations are estimated from present-day carriers, the path between them is the least-cost route over terrain. Dates are node TMRCAs, good to centuries. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.

The line moved fastest 4,400–3,000 years ago: 1,100 km, about 780 km per millennium. From the root of the letter on, the line moved slowest 42,100–35,000 years ago: 530 km in 7,100 years. In all, the line covered 11,200 km along Discover's route. Joining the ancestors with straight lines gives 8,600 km; a straight line from the first point to the branch gives 3,800 km.
