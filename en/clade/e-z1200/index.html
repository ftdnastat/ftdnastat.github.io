E-Z1200 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,276 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Morocco (27% of kits), Algeria (21% of kits), Libya (19% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,600 to 3,500 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-FGC22807, split off about 4,500 years ago by Discover. YFull has 656 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,600 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M81 branch, which has a note.
