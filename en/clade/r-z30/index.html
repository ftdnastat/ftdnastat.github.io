R-Z30 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,538 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-Z2, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Netherlands (7.0%); England (5.1%); Flanders, Belgium (8.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,600 to 4,800 years ago. YFull has 535 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,800 years ago.

Belongs to the R-U106 branch, which has a note.

How the branch's ancestral line moved, as modelled by FamilyTreeDNA Discover: ancestor locations are estimated from present-day carriers, the path between them is the least-cost route over terrain. Dates are node TMRCAs, good to centuries. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.
