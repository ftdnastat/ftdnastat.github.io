R-Z30 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,528 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-Z2, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Netherlands (7.0%); England (5.1%); Flanders, Belgium (8.2%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,600 to 4,800 years ago. YFull has 535 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,800 years ago.

Belongs to the R-U106 branch, which has a note.
