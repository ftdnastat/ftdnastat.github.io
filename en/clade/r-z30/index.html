R-Z30 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,522 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-Z2, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Netherlands (7.0% of kits), England (5.1% of kits), Belgium (Flanders) (8.2% of kits).
