G-Z727 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,933 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Switzerland (4.6%), France (2.0%), Croatia (2.6%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,900 to 5,200 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, G-Z6901, split off about 5,900 years ago by Discover. YFull has 302 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

Belongs to the G-P303 branch, which has a note.
