G-CTS574 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,795 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of Adygea) (34% of kits), Georgia (Republic of Abkhazia) (29% of kits), Russia (Republic of Kabardino-Balkaria) (18% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 16k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago. YFull has 1,569 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.

Belongs to the G-P15 branch, which has a note.
