Haplogroup G-L140 G2a-L140

Path from the root:G-M201 28k years›G-P15 19k years›G-L1259 19k years›G-P303 12k years›G-L140

G-L140 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,210 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-PF3345, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Adygea, Russia (33%); Abkhazia, Georgia (28%); Kabardino-Balkaria, Russia (16%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 12k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 9,800 to 13k years ago. YFull has 1,100 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 11k years ago.

Belongs to the G-P303 branch, which has a note.
