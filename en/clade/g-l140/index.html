G-L140 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,194 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-PF3345, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of Adygea) (34% of kits), Georgia (Republic of Abkhazia) (28% of kits), Russia (Republic of Kabardino-Balkaria) (17% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 12k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 9,800 to 13k years ago. YFull has 1,092 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 11k years ago.
