Haplogroup E-M4901

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-V38 41k years›E-M2 18k years›E-M4901

E-M4901 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,230 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-M4895, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Ghana (59% of kits), Jamaica (40% of kits), Kenya (35% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 16k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago. YFull has 1,125 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.

Belongs to the E-M2 branch, which has a note.
