E-M4901 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,228 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-M4895, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Ghana (59% of kits), Jamaica (40% of kits), Kenya (35% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 16k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago. YFull has 1,119 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.
