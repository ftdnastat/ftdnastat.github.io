Haplogroup G-P15 G2a

Path from the root:G-M201 28k years›G-L156 23k years›G-P15

G-P15 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 7,558 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Adygea, Russia (42%); North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (44%); Abkhazia, Georgia (40%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 19k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 17k to 22k years ago. YFull has 2,087 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 19k years ago.

The Russian Wikipedia says G2a makes up 5–10% of populations in Mediterranean European countries and is very rare in northern Europe. The English Wikipedia reports G2a in 20 of 22 ancient Y-DNA samples from Treilles, France, and places the Iceman, Ötzi, in G2a2b.
