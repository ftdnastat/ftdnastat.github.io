I-L1302 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 997 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-M6155, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Sweden (5.9%), Norway (2.8%), Finland (0.9%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 2,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 1,700 to 2,300 years ago. YFull has 287 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 1,800 years ago.
