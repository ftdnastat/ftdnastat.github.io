R-U106 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 25,674 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Netherlands (27% of kits), Belgium (Flanders) (30% of kits), England (20% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,400 to 5,700 years ago. YFull has 2,302 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,600 years ago.

The R-U106 project at FamilyTreeDNA says U106 descends from an R-L151 group located near the Yamnaya culture, and that it gained strength in what is now Germany probably a little before 3000 BC. The project finds it most significant in Germany and its neighbours, Scandinavia and Britain. Wikipedia adds that U106 appears to account for over a quarter of R1b in Europe.
