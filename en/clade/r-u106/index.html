Haplogroup R-U106 R1b-U106

Path from the root:R-M207 28k years›R-M173 22k years›R-M343 20k years›R-M269 6,500 years›R-L23 6,400 years›R-L51 6,100 years›R-L151 5,000 years›R-U106

R-U106 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 25,723 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Netherlands (27%); Flanders, Belgium (29%); England (20%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,400 to 5,700 years ago. YFull has 2,302 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,600 years ago.

The R-U106 project at FamilyTreeDNA says U106 descends from an R-L151 group located near the Yamnaya culture, and that it gained strength in what is now Germany probably a little before 3000 BC. The project finds it most significant in Germany and its neighbours, Scandinavia and Britain. Wikipedia adds that U106 appears to account for over a quarter of R1b in Europe.
