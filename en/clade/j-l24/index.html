J-L24 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,293 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-L25, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Tunisia (11% of kits), Azerbaijan (8.6% of kits), Bahrain (7.6% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 14k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 12k to 16k years ago. YFull has 1,205 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 14k years ago.

Belongs to the J-M410 branch, which has a note.
