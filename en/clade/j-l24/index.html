Haplogroup J-L24 J2a-L24

Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M172 28k years›J-M410 20k years›J-L26 17k years›J-PF5160 16k years›J-L24

J-L24 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,295 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-L25, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Tunisia (11%), Azerbaijan (8.6%), Bahrain (7.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 14k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 12k to 16k years ago. YFull has 1,205 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 14k years ago.

Luis et al. (2021) count L24 among the markers associated with the Neolithic agricultural revolution. Among the Basques, they found it to be rare: 3.8% of men in Álava.
