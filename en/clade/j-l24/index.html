J-L24 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,290 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-L25, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. The branch's common ancestor lived about 14k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 12k to 16k years ago. YFull has 1,194 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 14k years ago.
