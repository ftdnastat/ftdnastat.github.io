I-Z61 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,798 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-Z60, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Sweden (11% of kits), Norway (7.7% of kits), Isle of Man (9.1% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,700 to 4,800 years ago.
