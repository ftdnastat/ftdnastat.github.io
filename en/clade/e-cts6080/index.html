E-CTS6080 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 775 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-L1250, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Palestine (5.9%); Sicily, Italy (5.7%); United Arab Emirates (3.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 8,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 7,000 to 9,200 years ago. YFull has 373 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,000 years ago.
