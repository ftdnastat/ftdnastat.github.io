Equivalent branches, the same kits: E-M215 E1b1b (14,690 kits, 35k years)

E-M35 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 14,690 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Somalia (67%), Eritrea (57%), Morocco (42%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 25k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 22k to 29k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-M215, split off about 35k years ago by Discover. YFull has 5,473 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 24k years ago.

Wikipedia says E-M35 is primarily distributed in North Africa and the Horn of Africa, with moderate frequencies in the Middle East, Europe and Southern Africa. The same article places five men from Taforalt, Morocco, who lived 15,100–13,900 years ago, in the E-M78 subclade.
