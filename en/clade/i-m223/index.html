Haplogroup I-M223 I2b

Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-P215 25k years›I-P214 19k years›I-M223

I-M223 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 8,245 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Northern Ireland (7.3%), Malta (11%), Netherlands (6.2%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 17k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 15k to 19k years ago. YFull has 1,392 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 16k years ago.

Wikipedia gives M223 as I2a1b1 in the 2019 ISOGG naming (I2a2a in 2014). It says studies put the age of the branch at about 12,300–14,600 years. M223 peaks in Germany and north-eastern Sweden, and exceeds 4% only in Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, Scotland and England.
