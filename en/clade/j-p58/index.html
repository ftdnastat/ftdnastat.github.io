J-P58 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 13,940 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-CTS9721, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Saudi Arabia (47% of kits), Yemen (49% of kits), Jordan (43% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 9,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 8,600 to 12k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, J-Z1874, split off about 12k years ago by Discover. YFull has 9,091 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 9,200 years ago.

Belongs to the J-M267 branch, which has a note.
