L-M76 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 332 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, L-M27, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: India (2.9%), Sri Lanka (4.0%), Pakistan (3.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 17k years ago.
