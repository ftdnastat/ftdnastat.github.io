E-CTS693 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 534 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-CTS4004, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Sudan (2.4%), Palestine (1.9%), Turkey (1.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 9,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 8,000 to 11k years ago. YFull has 277 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,800 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M78 branch, which has a note.
