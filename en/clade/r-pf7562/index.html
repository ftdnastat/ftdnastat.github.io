R-PF7562 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 533 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-BY1713, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Albania (3.6%), Belarus (1.7%), Greece (1.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,800 to 6,500 years ago. YFull has 171 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,400 years ago.
