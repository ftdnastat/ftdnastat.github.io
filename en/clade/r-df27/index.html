R-DF27 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 12,979 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Spain (Andalusia) (35% of kits), Cuba (29% of kits), Dominican Republic (30% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,400 years ago. YFull has 1,875 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

Citing a 2017 article, Wikipedia estimates DF27 at about 4,200 years old and places its development in north-eastern Iberia. The same article reports about 40% across the Iberian Peninsula, with spikes of 70% among Basques. Wikipedia gives 6–20% for France.
