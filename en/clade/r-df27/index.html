R-DF27 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 13,003 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Andalusia, Spain (35%); Cuba (29%); Spain (25%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,300 years ago. YFull has 1,875 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

Citing a 2017 article, Wikipedia estimates DF27 at about 4,200 years old and places its development in north-eastern Iberia. The same article reports about 40% across the Iberian Peninsula, with spikes of 70% among Basques. Wikipedia gives 6–20% for France.
