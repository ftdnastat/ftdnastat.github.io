N-Z1925 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,063 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, N-CTS1737, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Finland (44%); Karelia, Russia (35%); Estonia (5.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 2,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,400 to 3,200 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N-CTS2733, split off about 3,600 years ago by Discover. YFull has 768 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,700 years ago.
