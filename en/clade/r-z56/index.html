R-Z56 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 981 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-BY3548, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Luxembourg (4.6%), Italy (2.4%), Switzerland (1.7%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,900 to 5,200 years ago. YFull has 135 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,400 years ago.
