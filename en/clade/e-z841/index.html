E-Z841 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 970 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-Z849, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Belarus (4.7%), Costa Rica (7.9%), Lithuania (3.9%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 17k years ago. YFull has 295 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.
