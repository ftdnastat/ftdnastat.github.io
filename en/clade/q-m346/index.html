Haplogroup Q-M346

Path from the root:Q-M242 30k years›Q-MEH2 27k years›Q-M346

Q-M346 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,852 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Panama (66%), Mexico (30%), Guatemala (38%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 21k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 19k to 25k years ago. YFull has 571 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 20k years ago.

The Russian Wikipedia says the Q1b-M346/L56 subclade is typical of Scandinavia, and that sample BLE007 from Huseby Klev, Sweden (7900 BCE), carried M346. The English Wikipedia notes that M346 is found at low frequency in Europe, South Asia and West Asia.
