Path from the root:O-M175 32k years›O-F265

O-F265 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,038 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Indonesia (57%), Vietnam (40%), South Korea (36%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 31k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 27k to 36k years ago. YFull has 731 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 30k years ago.
