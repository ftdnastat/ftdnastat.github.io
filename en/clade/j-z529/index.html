J-Z529 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,784 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Kosovo (20%), Albania (16%), Oman (9.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, J-Z2411, split off about 16k years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,160 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 16k years ago.

Belongs to the J-M102 branch, which has a note.
