J-Z529 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,780 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Kosovo (20%), Albania (16%), Oman (9.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago.
