J-Z597 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,568 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Albania (14%); Kosovo (15%); Mordovia, Russia (7.4%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,000 to 5,300 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, J-Z2509, split off about 5,300 years ago by Discover. YFull has 594 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,400 years ago.
