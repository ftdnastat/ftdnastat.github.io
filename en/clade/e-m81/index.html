Haplogroup E-M81

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-M35 25k years›E-L19 14k years›E-M81

E-M81 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,515 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Morocco (30% of kits), Algeria (23% of kits), Libya (20% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,900 to 5,200 years ago. YFull has 663 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,200 years ago.

Wikipedia says E-M81 is concentrated in the Middle East and North Africa, and is most common among Berber-speaking and Maghrebi Arab populations. It gives differing origin estimates: about 13,000 years ago in the Near East or Africa, or about 7,000 years ago in north-west Africa. Guanche remains from El Hierro (10th century) show E-M81 at about 44%, it says.
