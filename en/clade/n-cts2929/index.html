N-CTS2929 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,705 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Estonia (33% of kits), Latvia (22% of kits), Finland (16% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,500 to 4,600 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N-CTS10760, split off about 4,600 years ago by Discover. YFull has 762 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,900 years ago.

Wikipedia calls this branch CTS2929/VL29 (N1a1a1a1a1a) and says it is found with high frequency among Lithuanians, Latvians and Estonians, and in several other northern and eastern populations. The Russian Wikipedia adds that an early sample from Krasnoyarsk Krai is basal to VL29 and Z1936.
