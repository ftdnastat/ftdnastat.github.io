J-PF5430 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 878 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-Z435, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Malta (5.3%), Bulgaria (3.2%), Tunisia (4.0%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,400 to 4,500 years ago. YFull has 220 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,500 years ago.

Belongs to the J-L24 branch, which has a note.
