G-PF3369 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 134 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-PF3355, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Netherlands (0.8%), Italy (0.2%), Sweden (0.1%).
