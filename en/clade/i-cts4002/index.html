I-CTS4002 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,829 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS10228, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Bosnia and Herzegovina (31%), Croatia (26%), Serbia (21%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,900 to 6,400 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-CTS10936, split off about 5,900 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,150 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,000 years ago.

Belongs to the I-P37 branch, which has a note.
