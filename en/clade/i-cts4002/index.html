I-CTS4002 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,820 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS10228, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Bosnia and Herzegovina (30% of kits), Croatia (27% of kits), Serbia (21% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,900 to 6,400 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-CTS10936, split off about 5,900 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,142 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,000 years ago.
