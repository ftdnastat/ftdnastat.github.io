Haplogroup I-L22 I1-L22

Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-M253 20k years›I-DF29 4,500 years›I-S6346 4,000 years›I-L22

I-L22 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 8,536 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Sweden (20%), Finland (19%), Norway (14%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,400 to 4,500 years ago. YFull has 1,345 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,900 years ago.

The Russian Wikipedia calls L22 (also S142) a very large Scandinavian branch of I1. It says L22 is widely distributed in Britain, especially on the east coast, and also occurs in Poland and Russia.
