G-FGC693 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 443 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-Z6700, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of North Ossetia-Alania) (38% of kits), Georgia (16% of kits), Russia (Republic of Karachay-Cherkessia) (20% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 8,100 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 7,100 to 9,400 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, G-Z6744, split off about 9,700 years ago by Discover. YFull has 304 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 9,700 years ago.
