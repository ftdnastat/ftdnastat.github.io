R-L21 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 37,883 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ireland (57%), Northern Ireland (49%), Scotland (39%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,700 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,100 to 5,300 years ago. YFull has 3,241 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

L21 is most frequent in Ireland and dominant in Scotland, Wales and Brittany. According to Wikipedia, it reached Britain and Ireland in the Early Bronze Age with the Bell Beaker culture, when people of steppe descent largely replaced the Neolithic population of Britain. L21 made up 90% of the samples from Britain of that period, among them the Companion buried beside the Amesbury Archer near Stonehenge.
