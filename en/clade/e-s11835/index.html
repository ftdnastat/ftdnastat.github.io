E-S11835 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,036 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-S11956, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Eritrea (26%), Yemen (11%), Saudi Arabia (4.2%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 8,100 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 7,100 to 9,300 years ago. YFull has 1,385 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 7,700 years ago.
