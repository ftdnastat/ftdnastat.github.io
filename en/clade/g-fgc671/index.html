G-FGC671 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 271 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-FGC713, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (37%); Georgia (10.0%); Kabardino-Balkaria, Russia (12%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,200 to 5,700 years ago. YFull has 197 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,000 years ago.

Belongs to the G-P15 branch, which has a note.
