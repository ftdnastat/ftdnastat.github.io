Equivalent branches, the same kits: R-Z289 (4,520 kits, 4,500 years)

R-Z284 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,520 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Isle of Man (35%), Norway (19%), Sweden (11%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,200 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,600 to 4,800 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-Z289, split off about 4,500 years ago by Discover. YFull has 693 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,200 years ago.

Wikipedia says R1a in Scandinavia is virtually composed only of the Z284 subclade. Citing Underhill et al. (2014), it adds that Z284 occurs in north-western Europe and peaks at about 20% in Norway.
