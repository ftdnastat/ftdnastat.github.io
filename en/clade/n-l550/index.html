N-L550 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,045 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Latvia (21%), Lithuania (15%), Estonia (17%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 2,600 to 3,400 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N-S23232, split off about 3,600 years ago by Discover. YFull has 508 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 2,900 years ago.

Belongs to the N-CTS2929 branch, which has a note.

How the branch's ancestral line moved, as modelled by FamilyTreeDNA Discover: ancestor locations are estimated from present-day carriers, the path between them is the least-cost route over terrain. Dates are node TMRCAs, good to centuries. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.
