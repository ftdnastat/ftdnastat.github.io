G-Z2022 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 527 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-Z6414, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Azerbaijan (1.3%), Czech Republic (0.8%), Germany (0.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,200 to 5,700 years ago. YFull has 130 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,200 years ago.

Belongs to the G-P303 branch, which has a note.
