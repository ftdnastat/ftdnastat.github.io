I-CTS8584 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 864 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-Z185, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Switzerland (1.4%), Netherlands (1.3%), Denmark (1.0%).
