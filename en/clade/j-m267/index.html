Haplogroup J-M267 J1

Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M267

J-M267 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 19,274 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Yemen (64% of kits), Russia (Republic of Dagestan) (56% of kits), Saudi Arabia (54% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 19k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 17k to 22k years ago. YFull has 9,915 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 19k years ago.

Wikipedia says J-M267 reaches very high frequencies in Sudan and is uncommon in most of northern and central Europe; in North Africa it is dominated by P58. It names a Caucasus hunter-gatherer from Satsurblia Cave, Georgia, as the oldest carrier found so far. Di Giacomo et al. (2004) estimated the age at 17,000–24,000 years.
