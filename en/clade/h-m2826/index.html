Haplogroup H-M2826

Path from the root:H-L901 46k years›H-M2826

H-M2826 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 681 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, H-M69, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Sri Lanka (25%), India (19%), Bangladesh (22%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 45k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 40k to 52k years ago. YFull has 244 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 44k years ago.
