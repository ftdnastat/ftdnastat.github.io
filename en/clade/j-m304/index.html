Haplogroup J-M304 J

J-M304 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 36,819 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Yemen (72% of kits), Russia (Republic of Ingushetia) (76% of kits), Russia (Republic of Chechnya) (69% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 30k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 26k to 34k years ago. YFull has 15,301 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 32k years ago.
