R-DF19 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,473 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Luxembourg (4.6%), Belgium (2.9%), Germany (1.6%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,000 to 5,300 years ago. YFull has 142 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

The R-DF19 project at FamilyTreeDNA calls DF19 a subclade of P312 and says it may account for about 6–10% of all P312. According to the project, the mutation arose in a man from a Corded Ware community, most likely in Bohemia, born around 2600 BC.
