Haplogroup O-M175 O

O-M175 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,738 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Taiwan (87%), Vietnam (85%), South Korea (75%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 32k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 28k to 36k years ago. YFull has 1,721 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 31k years ago.

The Russian Wikipedia says haplogroup O is concentrated in East and Southeast Asia, with high frequencies among Chinese, Koreans, Japanese and Filipinos, and is nearly absent from Europe, Africa, the Americas and the Middle East. Hallast et al. (2023) date its origin to about 45,300 years ago.
