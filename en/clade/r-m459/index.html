R-M459 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 22,457 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-M198, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Poland (37% of kits), Czech Republic (34% of kits), Slovakia (35% of kits).
