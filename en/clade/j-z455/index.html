J-Z455 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 478 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-L210, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Moldova (3.1%), Belarus (1.9%), Ukraine (1.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 11k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 9,200 to 13k years ago. YFull has 92 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 11k years ago.

Belongs to the J-M67 branch, which has a note.
