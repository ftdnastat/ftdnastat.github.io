E-Z1711 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,180 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-U174, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Ghana (22%), Jamaica (14%), Nigeria (12%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,900 to 6,500 years ago. YFull has 476 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 6,200 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M2 branch, which has a note.
