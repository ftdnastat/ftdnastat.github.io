E-V12 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 922 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Somalia (60%), Ethiopia (12%), Sudan (7.8%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 11k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 9,000 to 12k years ago. YFull has 593 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 11k years ago.

Migration path #

How the branch's ancestral line moved, as modelled by FamilyTreeDNA Discover: ancestor locations are estimated from present-day carriers, the path between them is the least-cost route over terrain. Dates are node TMRCAs, good to centuries. The path ends at a fork of child branches: click one and the path continues into it.

The line moved fastest 14,600–13,400 years ago: 2,100 km, about 1,800 km per millennium. From the root of the letter on, the line moved slowest 42,100–35,000 years ago: 530 km in 7,100 years.
