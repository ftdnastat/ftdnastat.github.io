E-P147 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 20,081 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ghana (97%), Benin (96%), Mali (97%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 50k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 44k to 57k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-CTS9083, split off about 51k years ago by Discover. YFull has 6,766 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 52k years ago.
