I-L460 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 15,900 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Bosnia and Herzegovina (37% of kits), Croatia (34% of kits), Serbia (29% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 23k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 20k to 26k years ago. YFull has 3,412 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 21k years ago.
