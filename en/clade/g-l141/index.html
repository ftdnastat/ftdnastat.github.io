G-L141 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,703 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Adygea, Russia (33%); Abkhazia, Georgia (28%); Kabardino-Balkaria, Russia (18%).
