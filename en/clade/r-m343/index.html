R-M343 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 110,945 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-L761, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Ireland (80% of kits), Northern Ireland (75% of kits), Scotland (71% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 20k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 17k to 22k years ago.
