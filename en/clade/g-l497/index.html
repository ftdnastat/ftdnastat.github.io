G-L497 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,066 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, G-CTS9737, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Switzerland (4.9%); Flanders, Belgium (5.1%); France (2.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 7,600 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 6,600 to 8,700 years ago. YFull has 330 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 7,300 years ago.

Belongs to the G-P303 branch, which has a note.
