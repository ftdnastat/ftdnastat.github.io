Haplogroup O-F117

Path from the root:O-M175 32k years›O-M122 29k years›O-F18 13k years›O-F117

O-F117 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 153 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, O-F60, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: China (7.2%), South Korea (4.7%), Japan (2.6%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 13k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 15k years ago. YFull has 228 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 13k years ago.

Belongs to the O-M122 branch, which has a note.
