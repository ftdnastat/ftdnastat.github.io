J-L859 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 475 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Egypt (4.3% of kits), Pakistan (4.1% of kits), Saudi Arabia (2.0% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 1,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 1,300 to 1,800 years ago. YFull has 158 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 1,400 years ago.

The Quraysh & Banu-Hashem project regards carriers of L859 as descendants of the Quraysh tribe. According to the project, Sayyids and Sharifs with well-known lines of descent tested L859+.
