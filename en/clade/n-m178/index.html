Haplogroup N-M178 N1a-M178

Path from the root:N-M231 23k years›N-L729 18k years›N-M178

Equivalent branches, the same kits: N-P105 (10,126 kits, 17k years)

N-M178 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 10,126 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Finland (63%); Karelia, Russia (58%); Estonia (39%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 14k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 16k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N-P105, split off about 17k years ago by Discover.

Wikipedia describes M178 as the N1a1a subclade, defined by M178 and P298, and says it reaches about 60% among Finns. The Russian Wikipedia reports it in 64% of the Kazakh Uak clan.
