N-M178 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 9,956 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Finland (63% of kits), Russia (Republic of Karelia) (57% of kits), Estonia (39% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 14k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 16k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N-P105, split off about 16k years ago by Discover.

Wikipedia describes M178 as the N1a1a subclade, defined by M178 and P298, and says it reaches about 60% among Finns. The Russian Wikipedia reports it in 64% of the Kazakh Uak clan.
