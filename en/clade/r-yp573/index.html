R-YP573 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 602 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-YP569, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Karelia, Russia (11%); Russia (3.7%); Belarus (2.7%).
