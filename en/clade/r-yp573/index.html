R-YP573 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 596 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, R-YP569, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of Karelia) (11% of kits), Russian Federation (3.7% of kits), Belarus (2.7% of kits). The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-YP577, split off about 3,800 years ago by Discover. YFull has 240 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,400 years ago.
