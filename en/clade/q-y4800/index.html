Path from the root:Q-M242 30k years›Q-M346 21k years›Q-Y4800

Q-Y4800 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 614 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Sweden (2.4%), Afghanistan (4.2%), Tajikistan (4.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 20k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 18k to 23k years ago.
