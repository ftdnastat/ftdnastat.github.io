Haplogroup D-F974 D

D-F974 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 303 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, D-M174, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Japan (28% of kits), China (6.4% of kits), Mongolia (3.3% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 62k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 54k to 71k years ago. YFull has 134 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 57k years ago.
