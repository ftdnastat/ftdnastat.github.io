Haplogroup C-V86

Path from the root:C-M216 48k years›C-V20 34k years›C-V86

C-V86 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 65 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, C-V3163, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Italy (0.1%), France (0.1%), Germany (0.0%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 32k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 28k to 37k years ago. YFull has 35 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 23k years ago.
