Path from the root:T-M184 28k years›T-Z19859 17k years›T-L454

T-L454 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 2,070 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, T-L208, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: United Arab Emirates (18%), Somalia (15%), Bahrain (10%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 13k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 14k years ago. YFull has 873 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 12k years ago.
