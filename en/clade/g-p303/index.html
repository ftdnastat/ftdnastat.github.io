Haplogroup G-P303 G2a-P303

Path from the root:G-M201 28k years›G-P15 19k years›G-L1259 19k years›G-L141 15k years›G-P303

G-P303 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,524 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Adygea, Russia (33%); Abkhazia, Georgia (28%); Kabardino-Balkaria, Russia (16%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 12k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 14k years ago. YFull has 1,176 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 12k years ago.

Wikipedia places P303 in G2a2b2a. It says its subclades are now concentrated in southern Russia and the Caucasus, with lower levels elsewhere in Europe and Southwest Asia.
