J-Y4036 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 979 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-Z467, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Belarus (2.0%), Moldova (3.1%), Ukraine (1.7%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 13k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 15k years ago. YFull has 246 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 12k years ago.
