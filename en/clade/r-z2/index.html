R-Z2 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 5,401 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Netherlands (6.9%); England (5.1%); Flanders, Belgium (8.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,300 to 4,400 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-Z27, split off about 4,000 years ago by Discover. YFull has 514 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,800 years ago.
