R-Z2124 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,954 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Karachay-Cherkessia, Russia (31%); Adygea, Russia (25%); Kyrgyzstan (24%).
