R-Z2124 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,948 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Russia (Republic of Karachay-Cherkessia) (32% of kits), Russia (Republic of Adygea) (25% of kits), Kyrgyzstan (23% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,900 to 5,200 years ago. YFull has 1,124 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,300 years ago.

The Russian Wikipedia assigns ancient samples from Alan burials and from the Saltovo-Mayaki culture to Z2124.
