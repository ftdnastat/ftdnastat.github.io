J-FGC8703 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 384 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Egypt (3.9% of kits), Pakistan (3.9% of kits), Kuwait (1.9% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 1,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 1,200 to 1,700 years ago. YFull has 147 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 1,400 years ago.

The Quraysh & Banu-Hashem project regards carriers of FGC8703 as descendants of the Hashim clan. The project says the Hashimites came from the Hijaz in western Arabia and spread south to Yemen or northeast to the Levant, Mesopotamia and Persia.
