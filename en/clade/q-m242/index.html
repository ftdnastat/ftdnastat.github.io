Haplogroup Q-M242 Q

Q-M242 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,132 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Panama (66%); Mexico (32%); Native American, United States (32%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 30k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 26k to 34k years ago. YFull has 1,016 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 29k years ago.

Wikipedia puts the age of Q-M242 at about 15,000–25,000 years. It lists Anzick-1 (Clovis culture, 12,600 years ago, Q-L54*) and Kennewick Man (8,500 years, Q-M3) among the ancient Americas samples. The Russian Wikipedia calls Q1b1a1a-M3 typical of Indigenous Americans (over 90%).
