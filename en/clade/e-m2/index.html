Haplogroup E-M2 E-M2 (E1b1a)

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-V38 41k years›E-M2

E-M2 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,122 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Ghana (94% of kits), Benin (90% of kits), Nigeria (87% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 18k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 16k to 21k years ago. YFull has 1,129 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 17k years ago.

Wikipedia says E-M2 is primarily distributed in Africa, followed by West Asia, with the highest frequency and diversity in West Africa. YFull dates the common ancestor of present carriers to about 16,300 years ago. The Russian Wikipedia links its spread in southern and eastern Africa to the Bantu expansion.
