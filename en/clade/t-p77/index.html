T-P77 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 928 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: United Arab Emirates (16%), Bahrain (7.4%), Palestine (4.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,700 to 6,300 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, T-CTS6174, split off about 6,000 years ago by Discover. YFull has 340 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,200 years ago.
