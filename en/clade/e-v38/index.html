Haplogroup E-V38

Path from the root:E-M96 51k years›E-P147 50k years›E-P2 42k years›E-V38

E-V38 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,145 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-M2, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Ghana (94% of kits), Benin (90% of kits), Nigeria (87% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 40k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 35k to 46k years ago. YFull has 1,157 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 40k years ago.
