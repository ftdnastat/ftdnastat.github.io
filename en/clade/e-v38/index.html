Haplogroup E-V38

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-P2 42k years›E-V38

E-V38 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,170 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-M2, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Ghana (94%), Benin (90%), Nigeria (87%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 41k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 36k to 46k years ago. YFull has 1,163 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 40k years ago.

Belongs to the E-M96 branch, which has a note.
