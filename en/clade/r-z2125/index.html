R-Z2125 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 924 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Karachay-Cherkessia, Russia (27%); Adygea, Russia (21%); Kyrgyzstan (23%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,400 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,900 to 5,100 years ago. YFull has 870 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,300 years ago.

Wikipedia, citing Underhill and colleagues, says Z2125 is most frequent in Kyrgyzstan and among Afghan Pashtuns, at over 40% of men. According to Zhabagin and colleagues, every Kazakh man with R1a in earlier studies belonged to this sub-branch.
