Haplogroup E-M123

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-M35 25k years›E-CTS10298 20k years›E-PF1962 20k years›E-M123

E-M123 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,558 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Eritrea (26% of kits), Yemen (11% of kits), Belarus (8.4% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 18k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 16k to 21k years ago. YFull has 1,713 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 18k years ago.

Wikipedia names M34 as the main subclade of E-M123. It says the branch is found in Asia, Europe and Africa and is common in many Semitic-speaking communities, including Ashkenazi and Sephardic Jews. YFull (2019) puts the common ancestor about 18,000 years ago. Six Natufian remains from northern Israel carried E-Z830, a clade upstream of M123, per Lazaridis et al. (2016).
