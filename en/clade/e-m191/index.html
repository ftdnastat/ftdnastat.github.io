E-M191 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,487 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ghana (36%), Kenya (30%), Cameroon (22%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 5,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,100 to 6,800 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, E-P253, split off about 6,700 years ago by Discover. YFull has 475 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,300 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M2 branch, which has a note.
