Equivalent branches, the same kits: R-Z283 (15,160 kits, 4,800 years)

R-Z282 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 15,160 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Czech Republic (30%), Poland (29%), Isle of Man (35%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,200 to 5,400 years ago. YFull has 4,103 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,000 years ago.

Citing Pamjav et al. (2012), Wikipedia says Z282 appears to encompass most of the R1a found in Europe. It states that Z282* occurs in northern Ukraine, Belarus and Russia at about 20%. Underhill et al. (2014) date the split of Z282 and Z93 to about 5,800 years ago.
