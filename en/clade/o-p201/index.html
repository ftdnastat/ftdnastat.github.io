Haplogroup O-P201

Path from the root:O-M175 32k years›O-M122 29k years›O-M324 24k years›O-P201

O-P201 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 952 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Taiwan (56%), China (31%), Vietnam (30%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 24k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 21k to 27k years ago. YFull has 650 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 23k years ago.

Belongs to the O-M122 branch, which has a note.
