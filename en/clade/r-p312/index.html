R-P312 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 65,014 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ireland (65%), Northern Ireland (58%), Scotland (50%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 4,200 to 5,500 years ago. YFull has 7,014 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 4,500 years ago.

Wikipedia calls P312 one of the most common types of R-M269 in Europe, alongside U106. It says Western European populations are divided between P312 and U106. It cites Myres et al., who describe P312 as originating west of the Rhine basin and spreading from there.
