E-CTS229 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,153 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-U175, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Ghana (20% of kits), Jamaica (15% of kits), Trinidad and Tobago (16% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 8,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 7,800 to 11k years ago. YFull has 403 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,500 years ago.

Belongs to the E-M2 branch, which has a note.
