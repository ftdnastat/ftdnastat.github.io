Haplogroup G-M201 G

G-M201 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 10,279 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Adygea, Russia (44%); North Ossetia-Alania, Russia (48%); Abkhazia, Georgia (42%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 28k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 25k to 32k years ago. YFull has 2,394 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 26k years ago.

Wikipedia says G-M201 is widely thought to have originated in Western Asia and is now most common among ethnic groups of the Caucasus. Digora, North Ossetia, has the highest known concentration of G in a single city. It has also been found in Neolithic Anatolia (Boncuklu, 8300–7600 BCE); Hallast et al. (2021) give an origin of more than 31,100 years ago.
