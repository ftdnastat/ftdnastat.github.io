Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-P215

I-P215 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 16,712 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS2257, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Bosnia and Herzegovina (37%), Croatia (35%), Serbia (29%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 25k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 22k to 28k years ago.

Belongs to the I-M170 branch, which has a note.
