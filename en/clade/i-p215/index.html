Haplogroup I-P215

Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-P215

I-P215 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 16,669 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS2257, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Bosnia and Herzegovina (37% of kits), Croatia (35% of kits), Serbia (29% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 25k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 22k to 28k years ago.
