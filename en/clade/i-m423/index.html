Haplogroup I-M423

Path from the root:I-M170 28k years›I-P37 20k years›I-M423

I-M423 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,828 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, I-CTS5375, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. It is most common in these samples: Bosnia and Herzegovina (33% of kits), Croatia (28% of kits), Serbia (23% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 17k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 15k to 20k years ago. YFull has 1,370 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.
