Haplogroup R-M269 R1b-M269

Path from the root:R-M207 28k years›R-M173 22k years›R-M343 20k years›R-P297 15k years›R-M269

R-M269 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 109,204 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ireland (80%), Northern Ireland (75%), Scotland (70%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 6,500 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,700 to 7,400 years ago. YFull has 11,069 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 6,400 years ago.

Wikipedia calls M269 the most common Y-DNA lineage among European males and estimates that about 110 million men in Europe carry it. It adds that all seven Yamnaya-culture males tested belonged to M269. Olalde et al. (2017) trace its sudden appearance in Western Europe about 5,000–4,500 years ago to the spread of the Beaker culture.
