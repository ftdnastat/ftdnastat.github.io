N-L708 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 9,354 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Finland (60%); Karelia, Russia (56%); Estonia (38%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 7,800 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 6,800 to 8,900 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, N-F1419, split off about 12k years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,845 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 7,900 years ago.

Belongs to the N-M178 branch, which has a note.
