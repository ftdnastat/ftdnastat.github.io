J-L620 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 14,406 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Yemen (51%), Saudi Arabia (48%), Kuwait (41%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 13k to 17k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, J-PF4832, split off about 15k years ago by Discover. YFull has 9,299 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.

The English Wikipedia, citing Skourtanioti et al. (2020), says that one of four men from Tell Kurdu who lived around 5661–5630 BC belonged to L620.
