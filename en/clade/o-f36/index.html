Path from the root:O-M175 32k years›O-M122 29k years›O-F36

O-F36 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,187 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, O-M324, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Taiwan (58%), China (41%), Philippines (30%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 29k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 25k to 33k years ago.
