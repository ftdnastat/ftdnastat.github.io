Haplogroup E-M96 E

E-M96 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 20,301 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Ghana (97% of kits), Benin (98% of kits), Senegal (97% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 52k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 45k to 59k years ago. YFull has 6,778 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 53k years ago.
