Path from the root:T-M184 28k years›T-L906 6,300 years›T-CTS8603

T-CTS8603 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 952 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, T-P77, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: United Arab Emirates (16%), Bahrain (7.4%), Palestine (4.5%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 6,000 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 5,200 to 6,900 years ago. YFull has 351 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,600 years ago.
