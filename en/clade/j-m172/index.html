Haplogroup J-M172 J2

Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M172

J-M172 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 17,274 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ingushetia, Russia (65%); Chechnya, Russia (43%); Georgia (27%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 28k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 24k to 32k years ago. YFull has 5,386 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 28k years ago.

Wikipedia says J-M172 is typical of the Near East, Southern Europe, Southwest Asia and the Caucasus. Batini et al. (2015) estimated its age at 19,000–24,000 years. YFull dates the common ancestor of present carriers to about 28,000 years ago.
