I-M26 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 928 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Chuvashia, Russia (4.6%); Chile (4.9%); Spain (2.2%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 12k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 11k to 14k years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, I-CTS413, split off about 16k years ago by Discover. YFull has 230 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 12k years ago.

Belongs to the I-P37 branch, which has a note.
