Haplogroup B-M181 B

B-M181 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 486 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Cameroon (4.5%), Sudan (3.9%), Nigeria (4.1%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 86k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 75k to 98k years ago. YFull has 203 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 85k years ago.
