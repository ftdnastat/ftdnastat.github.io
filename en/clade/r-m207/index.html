Haplogroup R-M207 R

R-M207 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 134,727 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Ireland (82% of kits), Northern Ireland (79% of kits), Scotland (76% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 28k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 24k to 32k years ago. YFull has 17,991 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 29k years ago.
