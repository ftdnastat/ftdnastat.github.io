Equivalent branches, the same kits: R-PF6155 (4,051 kits, 4,600 years)

R-M458 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 4,051 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Czech Republic (18%), Poland (15%), Slovakia (12%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 4,300 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,700 to 4,900 years ago. The oldest of the equivalent branches, R-PF6155, split off about 4,600 years ago by Discover. YFull has 1,221 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 5,000 years ago.

Wikipedia calls M458 a mainly Slavic branch. It is found roughly from the Rhine basin to the Urals. Wikipedia adds that West Slavs and Hungarians have a lot of M458 and little of the neighbouring Z92. The Russian Wikipedia says that among the branches of R1a, M458 is the one typical of Slavic and Baltic peoples.
