J-PF5043 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 256 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-PF5056, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Tajikistan (2.9%); Lebanon (1.9%); Mordovia, Russia (2.6%).
