Haplogroup E-M34

Path from the root:E-M96 52k years›E-P147 50k years›E-M35 25k years›E-CTS10298 20k years›E-M123 18k years›E-M34

E-M34 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 3,404 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. It is most common in these samples: Eritrea (26% of kits), Yemen (11% of kits), Belarus (8.1% of kits). The branch's common ancestor lived about 15k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 14k to 18k years ago. YFull has 1,686 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 15k years ago.

Wikipedia names M34 as the dominant subclade of E-M123. Its frequency table has the highest figure, 31.1%, for Jordanians from the Dead Sea area (Flores et al., 2005). In the origin section it says M34 seems to be characteristic of Semitic speakers in Ethiopia.
