J-PF5141 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,045 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-Z504, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. YFull has 382 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,300 years ago.
