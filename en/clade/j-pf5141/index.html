J-PF5141 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 1,051 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, J-Z504, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Montenegro (4.5%), Armenia (2.8%), Belarus (2.4%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 7,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 6,800 to 9,000 years ago. YFull has 382 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 8,300 years ago.

Belongs to the J-M67 branch, which has a note.
