O-M133 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 171 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, O-F149, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Taiwan (9.5%), China (5.1%), South Korea (5.1%).
