Haplogroup J-M410 J2a

Path from the root:J-M304 30k years›J-M172 28k years›J-M410

J-M410 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 11,395 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Stands out most: Ingushetia, Russia (64%); Chechnya, Russia (37%); Azerbaijan (22%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 20k years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 18k to 23k years ago. YFull has 4,223 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 19k years ago.

Wikipedia says J-M410* is found in Georgia and North Ossetia. It reports ancient J-Y12379 in a Mesolithic sample from Kotias Klde Cave, Georgia, and J-PF5008 from the Hotu and Kamarband caves in Iran, dated 9,100–8,600 BCE. Both samples, it says, belong to the Trialetian culture.
