E-CTS6377 is a branch of the FamilyTreeDNA Y tree with 822 kits that gave a country, counting all sub-branches. Almost all of its kits sit in a single sub-branch, E-CTS2001, so this branch gets no conclusions of its own. Stands out most: Albania (11%), Bosnia and Herzegovina (6.8%), Montenegro (6.7%). The branch's common ancestor lived about 3,900 years ago: the FTDNA Discover estimate runs from 3,400 to 4,500 years ago. YFull has 311 samples in this branch and dates its common ancestor to 3,900 years ago.

Belongs to the E-V13 branch, which has a note.
