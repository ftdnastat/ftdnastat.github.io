Brazil: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Brazil: 733 kits with a Y-DNA result and 452 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-ZZ12_1* — 9%, R1b-L151 — 9% and E-PF2546 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are B2 — 10%, L2a — 8% and C1b — 8%. YFull has 125 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 1% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
