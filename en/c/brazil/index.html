Brazil: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Brazil: 733 kits with a Y-DNA result and 451 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 45%, E1b1b-M35 — 13% and J2 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are B2 — 14%, H — 12% and L3 — 10%. YFull has 125 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 31% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
