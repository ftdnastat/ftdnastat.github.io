Brazil: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 735 kits with a Y-DNA result and 452 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 45%, E1b1b-M35 — 13% and J2 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are B2 — 14%, H — 12% and L3 — 10%. YFull has 127 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J: 13% on FTDNA against 22% on YFull.
