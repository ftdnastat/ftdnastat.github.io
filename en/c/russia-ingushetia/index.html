Russia (Republic of Ingushetia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Ingushetia): 119 kits with a Y-DNA result and 67 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Ingushetia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are J2a-Z7671 — 61%, L-M357 — 12% and J1 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 31%, H — 28% and M1a1b1b — 7%. YFull has 195 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J2a-Z7671: 61% on FTDNA against 85% on YFull.
