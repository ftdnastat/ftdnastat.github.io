Vietnam: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 128 kits with a Y-DNA result and 61 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are O2 — 39%, O1b — 35% and O1a — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are M — 39%, F1a1a — 11% and B4 — 7%.
