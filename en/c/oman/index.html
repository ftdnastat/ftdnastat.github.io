Oman: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Oman: 478 kits with a Y-DNA result and 39 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC2 — 14%, J1-L858 — 10% and J-Z529* — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 409 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 14% on FTDNA against 27% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
