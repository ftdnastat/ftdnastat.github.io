Oman: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Oman: 481 kits with a Y-DNA result and 39 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1-L858 — 34%, J2 — 19% and E1b1b-M35 — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 409 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-P58: 41% on FTDNA against 53% on YFull.
