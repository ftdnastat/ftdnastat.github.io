Albania: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Albania: 278 kits with a Y-DNA result and 58 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-V13 — 28%, J2 — 22% and R1b-M269 — 17%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 48%, U — 14% and J1c — 7%. YFull has 678 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1b-M269: 17% on FTDNA against 24% on YFull.
