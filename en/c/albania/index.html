Albania: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Albania: 277 kits with a Y-DNA result and 58 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-Z1296 — 14%, E-BY3880* — 12% and E-S2979 — 12%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 7%, H — 5% and H12a — 5%. YFull has 678 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-S2077: 6% on FTDNA against 1% on YFull.
