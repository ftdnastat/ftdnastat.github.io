Bashkortostan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 104 kits with a Y-DNA result and 23 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Bashkortostan are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russia page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 27%, N1a-M178 — 18% and R1b-Z2106 — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 100 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1b-M73: 6% on FTDNA against 0% on YFull.
