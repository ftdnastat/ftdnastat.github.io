Italy (Sicily): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Italy (Sicily): 123 kits with a Y-DNA result and 67 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Italy (Sicily) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Italy page. On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 21%, E-M78 — 18% and R1b-P312 — 15%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 42%, U — 18% and HV — 7%. YFull has 87 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R: 29% on FTDNA against 21% on YFull.
