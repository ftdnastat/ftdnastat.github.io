Italy (Sicily): summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Italy (Sicily): 122 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-V22 — 9%, E-BY3880* — 5% and J-Z1847* — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 7%, U6a — 6% and D4j — 3%. YFull has 87 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is J-Z1296: 2% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
