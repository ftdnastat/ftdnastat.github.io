Australia (Aboriginal Australian): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 34 kits with a Y-DNA result and 30 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Australia (Aboriginal Australian) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Australia page. That is too few for a summary: with fewer than 50 kits the percentages are shaky, and the site draws no conclusions from them.
