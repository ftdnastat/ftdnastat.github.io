Russia (Republic of Kabardino-Balkaria): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Kabardino-Balkaria): 84 kits with a Y-DNA result and 9 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Kabardino-Balkaria) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are G2a — 36%, J2 — 18% and R1a-M198 — 13%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 244 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is G2a-L1264: 6% on FTDNA against 23% on YFull.
