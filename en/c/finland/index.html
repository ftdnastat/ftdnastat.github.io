Finland: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Finland: 10,026 kits with a Y-DNA result and 7,445 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-L258 — 16%, N-Z1941 — 13% and N-Z5894 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are U5b — 13%, H1a — 6% and U5a — 6%. YFull has 1,294 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
