Finland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Finland: 10,202 kits with a Y-DNA result and 7,470 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are N-Z1933 — 42%, I1-CTS6868 — 17% and N-CTS2929 — 16%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U5b — 13% and U5a — 6%. YFull has 1,294 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R: 10% on FTDNA against 14% on YFull.
