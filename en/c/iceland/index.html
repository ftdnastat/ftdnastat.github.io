Iceland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Iceland: 198 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-M269 — 41%, I1 — 27% and R1a-M198 — 23%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 34%, U — 18% and J1c7 — 6%.
