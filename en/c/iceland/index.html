Iceland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Iceland: 198 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-M269 — 21%, I-S63* — 19% and R1a-M198 — 16%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 10%, U5a — 10% and H2a — 9%.
