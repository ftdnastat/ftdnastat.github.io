China: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for China: 1,072 kits with a Y-DNA result and 310 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are O-M324 — 40%, O — 25% and C2 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are M7b — 8%, F1a — 5% and B4c — 5%. YFull has 1,396 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is O-M324: 40% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
