China: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for China: 1,072 kits with a Y-DNA result and 310 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are O2 — 45%, O1b — 9% and C2 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are M — 22%, B4 — 14% and F1 — 9%. YFull has 1,396 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is O: 70% on FTDNA against 52% on YFull.
