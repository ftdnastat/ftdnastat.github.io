Slovenia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Slovenia: 455 kits with a Y-DNA result and 255 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-CTS3402* — 10%, I-Y3548 — 10% and R-Y2898 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 9%, U5a — 5% and H2a — 4%. YFull has 52 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is I-Z2040: 3% on FTDNA against 21% on YFull.
