Ghana: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 423 kits with a Y-DNA result and 14 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are E-M191 — 36% and E-U175 — 20%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
