United Arab Emirates: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 1,371 kits with a Y-DNA result and 168 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1-L858 — 29%, T-CTS6507 — 16% and J2 — 15%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 15%, H — 13% and J1b — 10%. YFull has 664 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E: 12% on FTDNA against 25% on YFull.
