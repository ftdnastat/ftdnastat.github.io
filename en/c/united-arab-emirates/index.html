United Arab Emirates: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for United Arab Emirates: 1,354 kits with a Y-DNA result and 166 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are T-Z709 — 16%, J-FGC1721* — 11% and J-FGC2 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are J1b — 10%, R0a — 7% and K2a — 6%. YFull has 661 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is T-Z709: 16% on FTDNA against 10% on YFull.
