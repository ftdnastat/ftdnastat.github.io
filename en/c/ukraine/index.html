Ukraine: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Ukraine: 4,411 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,132 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-S20602* — 7%, I-Y3548 — 4% and R-L1029 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 11%, J1c — 6% and U5a — 4%. YFull has 597 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
