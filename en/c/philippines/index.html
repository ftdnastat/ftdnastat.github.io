Philippines: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Philippines: 413 kits with a Y-DNA result and 188 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are O2 — 34%, O1a — 17% and C — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are B4 — 26%, M7c1c3 — 13% and E1a — 12%. YFull has 30 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 2% on FTDNA against 20% on YFull.
