Malaysia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 61 kits with a Y-DNA result and 25 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are O2 — 30%, O1b — 23% and O1a — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
