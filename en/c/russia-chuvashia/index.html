Russia (Republic of Chuvashia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Chuvashia): 191 kits with a Y-DNA result and 69 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-S2979 — 12%, NO — 9% and J-PF5160* — 8%.
