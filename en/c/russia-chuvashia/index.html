Russia (Republic of Chuvashia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Chuvashia): 205 kits with a Y-DNA result and 71 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Chuvashia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z280 — 22%, J2a — 18% and N1a-M178 — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 32%, H — 28% and V13 — 7%. YFull has 39 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J2: 19% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
