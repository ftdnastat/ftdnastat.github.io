Russia (Republic of Chuvashia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Chuvashia): 214 kits with a Y-DNA result and 76 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Chuvashia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z280 — 22%, J2a — 17% and N1a-M178 — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 30%, U — 30% and T2 — 9%. YFull has 39 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J2: 18% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
