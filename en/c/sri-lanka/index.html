Sri Lanka: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 150 kits with a Y-DNA result and 33 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are H-M52 — 19%, R1a-M198 — 15% and R2 — 10%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
