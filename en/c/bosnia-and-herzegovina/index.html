Bosnia and Herzegovina: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Bosnia and Herzegovina: 368 kits with a Y-DNA result and 125 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are I2a-CTS10228 — 30%, R1a-Z282 — 17% and E-V13 — 14%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 41%, U — 17% and J1c — 7%. YFull has 217 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I2a-CTS10228: 30% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
