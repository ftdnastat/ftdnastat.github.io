Bosnia and Herzegovina: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Bosnia and Herzegovina: 365 kits with a Y-DNA result and 125 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-Y3548 — 29%, E-BY3880* — 7% and R-Y2898 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 7%, K1a — 6% and H5a — 5%. YFull has 217 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-Y3548: 29% on FTDNA against 39% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
