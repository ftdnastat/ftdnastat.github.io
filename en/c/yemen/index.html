Yemen: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Yemen: 1,613 kits with a Y-DNA result and 94 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 13%, J-FGC7638* — 12% and J-CTS9721* — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 14%, J2a — 6% and L0a — 5%. YFull has 1,341 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
