Yemen: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 1,617 kits with a Y-DNA result and 95 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 64%, E1b1b-M35 — 18% and J2 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are J — 13%, L3 — 12% and R0a1a — 8%. YFull has 1,348 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-P58: 49% on FTDNA against 57% on YFull.
