FamilyTreeDNA has 454 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are C2 — 49%, O2-M134 — 11% and N1a-M178 — 8%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
