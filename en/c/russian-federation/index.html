Russian Federation: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russian Federation: 7,184 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,807 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 30%, N1a-M178 — 12% and J2 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 35%, U — 17% and K1 — 9%. YFull has 1,481 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z282: 23% on FTDNA against 43% on YFull.
