Russian Federation: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russian Federation: 7,165 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,805 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-Z92 — 6%, R-Y2898 — 5% and R-L1029 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 7%, J1c — 6% and U5a — 5%. YFull has 1,481 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R-Y2898: 5% on FTDNA against 13% on YFull.
