Lebanon: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Lebanon: 671 kits with a Y-DNA result and 155 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 6%, E-V22 — 5% and J2 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are T1a — 7%, K1a — 6% and U1b — 5%. YFull has 154 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
