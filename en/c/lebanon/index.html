Lebanon: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Lebanon: 673 kits with a Y-DNA result and 155 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 23%, J2 — 23% and E1b1b-M35 — 17%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 28%, U — 17% and J1 — 10%. YFull has 159 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is E: 19% on FTDNA against 27% on YFull.
