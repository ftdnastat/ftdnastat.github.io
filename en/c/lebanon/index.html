Lebanon: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Lebanon: 671 kits with a Y-DNA result and 154 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 23%, J2 — 23% and E1b1b-M35 — 17%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 28%, U — 18% and J1 — 10%. YFull has 154 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC11: 9% on FTDNA against 17% on YFull.
