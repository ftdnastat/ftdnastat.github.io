United Kingdom: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for United Kingdom: 9,731 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,461 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 57%, I1 — 15% and I2b — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 45%, U — 14% and J1 — 8%. YFull has 235 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1b-M269: 60% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
