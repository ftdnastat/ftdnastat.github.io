United Kingdom: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for United Kingdom: 9,707 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,459 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 6%, R1b-L21 — 3% and I1 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 7%, K1a — 5% and T2b — 5%. YFull has 235 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is I-S7660: 1% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
