Italy: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Italy: 6,027 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,961 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 25%, J2 — 19% and E1b1b-M35 — 12%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 39%, U — 14% and J1 — 8%. YFull has 426 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 18% on FTDNA against 23% on YFull.
