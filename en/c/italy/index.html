Italy: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Italy: 6,019 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,957 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-U152 — 7%, J2 — 5% and R1b-L151 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 6%, H — 5% and K1a — 5%. YFull has 426 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J-Z1296: 1% on FTDNA against 4% on YFull.
