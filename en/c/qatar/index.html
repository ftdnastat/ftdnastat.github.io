Qatar: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 697 kits with a Y-DNA result and 83 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 44%, J2a — 18% and E1b1b-M35 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 17%, HV — 14% and H — 11%. YFull has 406 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-L858: 35% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
