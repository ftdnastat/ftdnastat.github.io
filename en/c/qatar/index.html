Qatar: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Qatar: 691 kits with a Y-DNA result and 82 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC7 — 9%, J1 — 8% and J2a-L25 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 16%, J1b — 10% and U1a — 6%. YFull has 401 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 6% on FTDNA against 12% on YFull.
