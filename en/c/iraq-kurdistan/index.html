Iraq (Kurdistan): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 54 kits with a Y-DNA result and 6 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Iraq (Kurdistan) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Iraq page. On the paternal line the most common are J2a — 28%, J1 — 24% and R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 13%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
