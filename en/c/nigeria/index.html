Nigeria: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 145 kits with a Y-DNA result and 19 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are E-M191 — 20%, E-U175 — 7% and B — 4%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
