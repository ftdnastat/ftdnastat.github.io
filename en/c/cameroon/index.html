FamilyTreeDNA has 246 kits with a Y-DNA result and 25 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are E-M2 (E1b1a) — 62%, R — 22% and B — 4%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
