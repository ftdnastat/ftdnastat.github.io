Iran: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Iran: 758 kits with a Y-DNA result and 177 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 25%, J1 — 19% and R1b-L23 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 27%, U — 20% and HV — 11%. YFull has 178 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J1-P58: 12% on FTDNA against 21% on YFull.
