Iran: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Iran: 756 kits with a Y-DNA result and 177 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are K-M526* — 7%, J2 — 6% and G — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are H13a — 6%, U1a — 6% and H14a — 5%. YFull has 178 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same.
