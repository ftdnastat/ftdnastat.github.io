Georgia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Georgia: 649 kits with a Y-DNA result and 71 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are G2a — 28%, J2 — 28% and R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 24%, U — 23% and T2 — 6%. YFull has 205 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is G2a1-Z6552: 17% on FTDNA against 33% on YFull.
