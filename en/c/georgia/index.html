Georgia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Georgia: 631 kits with a Y-DNA result and 70 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are G2a — 18%, J2 — 9% and G-Z6779 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 16%, K1a — 10% and U3b — 9%. YFull has 205 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
