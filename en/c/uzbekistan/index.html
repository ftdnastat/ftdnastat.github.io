Uzbekistan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 271 kits with a Y-DNA result and 30 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 15%, C2 — 11% and R1a-M198 — 10%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 36 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is C: 14% on FTDNA against 0% on YFull.
