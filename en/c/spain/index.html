Spain: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Spain: 3,936 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,462 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 8%, R-Z278 — 7% and R-ZZ12_1* — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are U5b — 5%, K1a — 3% and J1c — 3%. YFull has 68 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I-CTS595: 2% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
