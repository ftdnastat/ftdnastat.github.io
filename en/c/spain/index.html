Spain: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Spain: 3,940 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,463 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 46%, E1b1b-M35 — 10% and J2 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 34%, U — 12% and A2 — 7%. YFull has 68 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is J: 15% on FTDNA against 37% on YFull.
