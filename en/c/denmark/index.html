Denmark: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Denmark: 1,635 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,118 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 35%, I1 — 33% and R1a-M198 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 14% and T2 — 10%. YFull has 102 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 12% on FTDNA against 21% on YFull.
