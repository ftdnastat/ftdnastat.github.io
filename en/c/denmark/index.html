Denmark: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Denmark: 1,633 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,117 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-S63* — 5%, I1 — 4% and I-S7660 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 6%, H1c — 6% and T2b — 6%. YFull has 102 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I-Z2040: 2% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
