Canada: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Canada: 1,652 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,114 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 7%, R1b-L21 — 3% and R-S744 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are T2b — 4%, J1c — 4% and U5a — 4%. YFull has 147 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different.
