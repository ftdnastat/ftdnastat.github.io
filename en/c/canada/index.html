Canada: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Canada: 1,657 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,115 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 64%, I1-DF29 — 7% and R1a-M198 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 43%, U — 11% and T2 — 7%. YFull has 147 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R: 71% on FTDNA against 46% on YFull.
