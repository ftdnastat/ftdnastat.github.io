Spain (Andalusia) Y-DNA

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Spain (Andalusia): 79 kits with a Y-DNA result. Only kits that name Spain (Andalusia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Spain page. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 48% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
