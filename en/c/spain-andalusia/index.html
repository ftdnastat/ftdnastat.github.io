Spain (Andalusia): summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Spain (Andalusia): 79 kits with a Y-DNA result and 30 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-ZZ12_1* — 11%, E-PF2546 — 8% and E-V22 — 6%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 46 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J-Z1296: 4% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.
