Spain (Andalusia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Spain (Andalusia): 79 kits with a Y-DNA result and 30 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Spain (Andalusia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Spain page. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-DF27 — 35%, R1b-U152 — 10% and J2 — 10%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 46 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 48% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
