Russia (Republic of Adygea): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Adygea): 408 kits with a Y-DNA result and 22 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Adygea) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are G2a-L1266 — 32%, R1a-Z2123 — 20% and J2a — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 46 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is G2a-L1264: 25% on FTDNA against 43% on YFull.
