Russia (Republic of Adygea): summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Adygea): 392 kits with a Y-DNA result and 21 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are G-Z6779 — 34%, R1a-Z2124 — 24% and G2a — 8%.
