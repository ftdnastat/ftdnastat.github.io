Netherlands: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Netherlands: 2,302 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,411 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 4%, I1 — 4% and R1b-U106 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are H1a — 6%, K1a — 6% and J1c — 5%. YFull has 270 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I-Z2535: 2% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
