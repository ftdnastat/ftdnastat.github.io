Netherlands: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Netherlands: 2,309 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,411 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 48%, I1 — 18% and I2b — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 45%, U — 13% and K1 — 8%. YFull has 270 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R: 55% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
