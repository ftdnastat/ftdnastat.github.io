Korea, Republic of: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Korea, Republic of: 236 kits with a Y-DNA result and 133 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are O1b — 36%, O2 — 28% and C2 — 15%. On the maternal line the most common are D4 — 24%, B4 — 18% and G — 10%. YFull has 56 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is O: 75% on FTDNA against 64% on YFull.
