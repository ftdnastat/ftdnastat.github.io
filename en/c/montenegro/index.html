Montenegro: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 195 kits with a Y-DNA result and 63 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are I2a-CTS10228 — 22%, E-V13 — 20% and R1b-CTS7556 — 16%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 29% and J1c — 6%. YFull has 188 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I2a-CTS10228: 22% on FTDNA against 37% on YFull.
