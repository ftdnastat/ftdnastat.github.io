Montenegro: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Montenegro: 190 kits with a Y-DNA result and 63 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-Y3548 — 19%, R-CTS7822 — 17% and E-S2979 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are U5b — 8%, J1c — 6% and H4 — 5%. YFull has 187 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-Y3548: 19% on FTDNA against 34% on YFull.
