Montenegro: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Montenegro: 191 kits with a Y-DNA result and 63 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I2a-CTS10228 — 21%, E-V13 — 20% and R1b-CTS7556 — 17%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 29% and J1c — 6%. YFull has 187 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I2a-CTS10228: 21% on FTDNA against 36% on YFull.
