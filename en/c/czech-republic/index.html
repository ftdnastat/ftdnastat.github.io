Czech Republic: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Czech Republic: 2,364 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,194 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z282 — 30%, R1b-L51 — 21% and I1 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 40%, U — 19% and T2 — 9%. YFull has 252 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 10% on FTDNA against 16% on YFull.
