Czech Republic: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Czech Republic: 2,353 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,193 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-L1029 — 6%, R-YP256 — 6% and R-L260* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 7%, J1c — 6% and T2b — 5%. YFull has 252 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1b-Z49: 2% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
