Wales: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Wales: 2,203 kits with a Y-DNA result and 938 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 67%, I1 — 11% and E1b1b-M35 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 12% and K1 — 9%. YFull has 109 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1b-DF13: 26% on FTDNA against 43% on YFull.
