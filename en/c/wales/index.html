Wales: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Wales: 2,203 kits with a Y-DNA result and 935 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 10%, R-Z39589* — 7% and R1b-L21 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 7%, T2b — 6% and H1a — 5%. YFull has 109 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R-DF25: 1% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
