Norway: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Norway: 4,562 kits with a Y-DNA result and 3,859 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are I1-DF29 — 29%, R1b-L51 — 26% and R1a-Z284 — 19%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 43%, U — 17% and J1 — 9%. YFull has 456 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R: 52% on FTDNA against 45% on YFull.
