Norway: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Norway: 4,545 kits with a Y-DNA result and 3,850 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-L813 — 8%, R-Z287 — 7% and R-CTS4179 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 8%, U5a — 6% and U5b — 5%. YFull has 456 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-BY266: 5% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.
