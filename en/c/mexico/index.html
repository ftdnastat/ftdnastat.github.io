Mexico: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 2,297 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,801 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are Q-M3 — 25%, R1b-P312 — 24% and E1b1b-M35 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are A2 — 31%, B2 — 24% and C1 — 20%. YFull has 110 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is Q-M3: 25% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.
