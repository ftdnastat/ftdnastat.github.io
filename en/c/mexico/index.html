Mexico: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Mexico: 2,281 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,793 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are Q-M3 — 25%, R1b-L151 — 7% and R-ZZ12_1* — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are C1b — 10%, C1c — 8% and B2 — 5%. YFull has 110 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is Q-M3: 25% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
