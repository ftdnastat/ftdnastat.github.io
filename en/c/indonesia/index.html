Indonesia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Indonesia: 621 kits with a Y-DNA result and 84 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are O — 59%, O-M324 — 10% and K-M526* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are B5a — 13%, E1a — 10% and F1a — 10%. YFull has 30 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is O-M324: 10% on FTDNA against 20% on YFull.
