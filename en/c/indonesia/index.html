Indonesia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Indonesia: 621 kits with a Y-DNA result and 84 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are O1b-M95 — 55%, O2 — 12% and C — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are M — 38%, B4 — 12% and B5a1a — 11%. YFull has 30 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is O1b-M95: 55% on FTDNA against 10% on YFull.
