Russia (Republic of Tatarstan): summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Tatarstan): 114 kits with a Y-DNA result and 23 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are NO — 7%, N-Tat* — 7% and R-YP237 — 6%.
