Tatarstan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 117 kits with a Y-DNA result and 25 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Tatarstan are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russia page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 24%, N1a-M178 — 17% and R1b-U106 — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 277 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1a-M198: 24% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
