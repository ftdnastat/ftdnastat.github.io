Chechnya: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 1,710 kits with a Y-DNA result and 465 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Chechnya are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russia page. On the paternal line the most common are J2a-Z7671 — 33%, J1 — 25% and L-M357 — 19%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 23%, H — 22% and T2 — 14%. YFull has 1,065 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J2a-Z7671: 33% on FTDNA against 44% on YFull.
