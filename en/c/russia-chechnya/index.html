Russia (Republic of Chechnya): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Chechnya): 1,697 kits with a Y-DNA result and 462 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Chechnya) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are J2a-Z7671 — 33%, J1 — 25% and L-M357 — 19%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 23%, H — 22% and T2 — 14%. YFull has 1,059 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J2a-Z7671: 33% on FTDNA against 44% on YFull.
