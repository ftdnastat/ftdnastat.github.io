Russia (Republic of Chechnya): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Chechnya): 1,681 kits with a Y-DNA result and 457 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-CTS6619 — 26%, L-M22 — 19% and J1-Z1842 — 19%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 10%, K1a — 7% and I2 — 6%.
