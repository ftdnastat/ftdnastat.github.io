Sweden: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Sweden: 12,186 kits with a Y-DNA result and 9,324 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-BY266 — 8%, I-L813 — 5% and I1-L22 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 6%, J1c — 6% and H2a — 6%. YFull has 1,423 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-BY266: 8% on FTDNA against 15% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
