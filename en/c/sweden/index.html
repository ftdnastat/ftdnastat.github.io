Sweden: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Sweden: 12,208 kits with a Y-DNA result and 9,337 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I1-DF29 — 41%, R1b-L51 — 20% and R1a-Z282 — 14%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 18% and J1 — 7%. YFull has 1,423 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I1-Z59: 13% on FTDNA against 21% on YFull.
