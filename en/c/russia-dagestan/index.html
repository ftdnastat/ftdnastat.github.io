Russia (Republic of Dagestan): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Dagestan): 1,006 kits with a Y-DNA result and 52 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Dagestan) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are J1-Z1842 — 53%, J2a — 11% and R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 31%, T2 — 13% and T1a — 8%. YFull has 116 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is J1-CTS1460: 24% on FTDNA against 11% on YFull.
