Kazakhstan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Kazakhstan: 743 kits with a Y-DNA result and 84 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are C2 — 40%, G — 8% and O-M324 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 6%, G2a — 5% and J1c — 5%. YFull has 78 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z2124: 2% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.
