Bahrain: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Bahrain: 767 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are T-Z709 — 9%, J-FGC2 — 7% and J-FGC1721* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 10%, L3e — 6% and V — 6%. YFull has 393 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 7% on FTDNA against 12% on YFull.
