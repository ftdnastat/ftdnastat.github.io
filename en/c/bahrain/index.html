Bahrain: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Bahrain: 780 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 22%, J1-FGC11 — 19% and E1b1b-M35 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 12%, M — 7% and L3e — 6%. YFull has 393 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-L858: 24% on FTDNA against 32% on YFull.
