Kuwait: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Kuwait: 2,252 kits with a Y-DNA result and 157 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC1695* — 7%, J-FGC2 — 7% and J-FGC23310* — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 15%, J1b — 8% and T1b — 4%. YFull has 1,647 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 7% on FTDNA against 15% on YFull.
