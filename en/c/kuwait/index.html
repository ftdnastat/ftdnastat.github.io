Kuwait: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Kuwait: 2,266 kits with a Y-DNA result and 157 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1-FGC11 — 35%, E1b1b-M35 — 12% and J2a — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are U — 12%, H — 10% and R0a2 — 10%. YFull has 1,647 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC1707: 21% on FTDNA against 29% on YFull.
