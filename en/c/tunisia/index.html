Tunisia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Tunisia: 250 kits with a Y-DNA result and 49 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-PF2546 — 17%, J2a-L25 — 7% and J-FGC1* — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 87 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E-PF2546: 17% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
