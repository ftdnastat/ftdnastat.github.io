Tunisia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Tunisia: 252 kits with a Y-DNA result and 49 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are E1b1b-M35 — 33%, J2a — 18% and J1-FGC1707 — 17%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 87 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC1707: 17% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
