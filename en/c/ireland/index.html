Ireland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Ireland: 22,307 kits with a Y-DNA result and 10,160 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-DF13 — 54%, R1b-U106 — 6% and I1 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 43%, U — 14% and J1 — 9%. YFull has 984 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I: 13% on FTDNA against 25% on YFull.
