Ireland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Ireland: 22,268 kits with a Y-DNA result and 10,135 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 6%, R-DF21* — 5% and R-DF105* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 7%, U5a — 5% and K1a — 5%. YFull has 984 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I-L137: 2% on FTDNA against 5% on YFull.
