Saudi Arabia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Saudi Arabia: 12,006 kits with a Y-DNA result and 807 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1-FGC11 — 40%, E1b1b-M35 — 10% and J2a — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 18%, J1 — 15% and H — 10%. YFull has 7,121 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-P58: 47% on FTDNA against 55% on YFull.
