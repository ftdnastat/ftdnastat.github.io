Saudi Arabia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Saudi Arabia: 11,913 kits with a Y-DNA result and 807 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC1695* — 7%, J-FGC7 — 6% and J1 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 18%, J1b — 9% and J2a — 4%. YFull has 7,119 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
