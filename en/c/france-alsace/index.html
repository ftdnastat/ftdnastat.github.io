France (Alsace): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 75 kits with a Y-DNA result and 65 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name France (Alsace) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the France page. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L23 — 51%, I1-Z59 — 7% and I2 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 51%, U — 14% and W3 — 5%. YFull has 33 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R: 57% on FTDNA against 27% on YFull.
