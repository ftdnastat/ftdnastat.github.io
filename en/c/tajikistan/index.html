Tajikistan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 170 kits with a Y-DNA result and 7 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z93 — 21%, J2a — 14% and J1 — 6%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.
