Armenia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Armenia: 1,180 kits with a Y-DNA result and 224 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J2a — 20%, R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 20% and J1 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 29%, U — 16% and HV — 10%. YFull has 819 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J: 35% on FTDNA against 41% on YFull.
