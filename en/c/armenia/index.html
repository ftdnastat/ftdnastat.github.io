Armenia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Armenia: 1,175 kits with a Y-DNA result and 223 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 17%, J1-Z1842 — 4% and J2a-Z2227 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 5%, K1a — 5% and N1b — 4%. YFull has 819 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
