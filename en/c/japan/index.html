Japan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 529 kits with a Y-DNA result and 296 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are D1a2 (Japan) — 23%, O1b — 22% and O2 — 20%. On the maternal line the most common are D4 — 36%, M7 — 13% and B4 — 9%. YFull has 59 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is D1a2 (Japan): 23% on FTDNA against 37% on YFull.
