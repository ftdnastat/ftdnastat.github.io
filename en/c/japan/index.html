Japan: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Japan: 527 kits with a Y-DNA result and 296 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are O — 33%, CT — 28% and O-M324 — 18%. On the maternal line the most common are M7a — 8%, D4b — 8% and D4e — 7%. YFull has 59 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is C2: 7% on FTDNA against 3% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
