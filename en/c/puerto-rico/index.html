Puerto Rico: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Puerto Rico: 596 kits with a Y-DNA result and 625 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 47%, E1b1b-M35 — 14% and E-M2 (E1b1a) — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are A2 — 31%, C1b2 — 17% and L3 — 9%. YFull has 44 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R: 53% on FTDNA against 36% on YFull.
