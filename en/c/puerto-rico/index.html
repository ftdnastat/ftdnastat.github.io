Puerto Rico: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Puerto Rico: 591 kits with a Y-DNA result and 621 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 10%, R-ZZ12_1* — 8% and R-Z278 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are C1b — 25%, A2k — 10% and A2z — 8%. YFull has 44 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is E-U175: 3% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
