Jordan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Jordan: 362 kits with a Y-DNA result and 28 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 54%, J2 — 16% and E-M78 — 6%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 188 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC11: 32% on FTDNA against 48% on YFull.
