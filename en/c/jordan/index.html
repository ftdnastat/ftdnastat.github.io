Jordan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 362 kits with a Y-DNA result and 28 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 55%, J2 — 15% and E-M78 — 6%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 187 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC11: 32% on FTDNA against 48% on YFull.
