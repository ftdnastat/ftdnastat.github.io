Jordan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Jordan: 361 kits with a Y-DNA result and 28 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC4422 — 15%, J1 — 12% and J-YSC0000076 — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 188 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 4% on FTDNA against 13% on YFull.
