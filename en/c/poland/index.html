Poland: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Poland: 7,353 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,392 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-YP256 — 6%, R-L1029 — 5% and R-Y2898 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 8%, J1c — 5% and U5a — 4%. YFull has 1,207 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R-Y2898: 4% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.
