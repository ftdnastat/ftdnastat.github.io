Poland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Poland: 7,381 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,395 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 37%, R1b-L51 — 11% and E1b1b-M35 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 40%, U — 15% and K1 — 9%. YFull has 1,207 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z282: 29% on FTDNA against 44% on YFull.
