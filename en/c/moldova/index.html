Moldova: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Moldova: 193 kits with a Y-DNA result and 117 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 18%, J1 — 16% and J2a — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 38%, K1a — 15% and U — 12%. YFull has 31 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1a-M458: 7% on FTDNA against 29% on YFull.
