Syrian Arab Republic: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Syrian Arab Republic: 908 kits with a Y-DNA result and 134 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 9%, J-FGC4422 — 9% and J-FGC1721* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 10%, N1b — 4% and J1b — 4%. YFull has 488 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 3% on FTDNA against 10% on YFull.
