Syrian Arab Republic: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Syrian Arab Republic: 914 kits with a Y-DNA result and 138 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 39%, J2 — 22% and E1b1b-M35 — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 22%, U — 15% and J1 — 10%. YFull has 488 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J1-L858: 26% on FTDNA against 49% on YFull.
