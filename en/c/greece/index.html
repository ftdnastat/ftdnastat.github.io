Greece: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Greece: 1,319 kits with a Y-DNA result and 462 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 20%, E1b1b-M35 — 19% and R1b-M269 — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 40%, U — 18% and T2 — 8%. YFull has 178 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I: 15% on FTDNA against 25% on YFull.
