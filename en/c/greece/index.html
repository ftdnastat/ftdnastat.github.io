Greece: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Greece: 1,318 kits with a Y-DNA result and 458 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 6%, I-S20602* — 5% and J1 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 7%, H — 5% and U5a — 5%. YFull has 178 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is I-Y3548: 1% on FTDNA against 4% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
