Palestinian Territory: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Palestinian Territory: 675 kits with a Y-DNA result and 70 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 42%, E1b1b-M35 — 19% and J2 — 15%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 21%, J — 11% and X — 6%. YFull has 240 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J2b: 6% on FTDNA against 15% on YFull.
