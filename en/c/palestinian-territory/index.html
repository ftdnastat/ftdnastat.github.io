Palestinian Territory: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Palestinian Territory: 642 kits with a Y-DNA result and 70 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 10%, J-FGC4422 — 9% and J-YSC0000076 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are R0a — 7%, T2d — 6% and M1a — 4%. YFull has 240 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC4422: 9% on FTDNA against 17% on YFull.
