United States (Native American): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 1,834 kits with a Y-DNA result and 2,352 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name United States (Native American) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the United States page. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 34%, Q-M3 — 23% and E-M2 (E1b1a) — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 15%, U — 11% and A2 — 11%.
