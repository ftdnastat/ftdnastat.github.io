Colombia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 304 kits with a Y-DNA result and 215 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 46%, Q-M3 — 11% and E1b1b-M35 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are A2 — 36%, B2d — 26% and C1 — 13%.
