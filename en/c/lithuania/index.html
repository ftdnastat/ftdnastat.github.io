Lithuania: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Lithuania: 1,864 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,065 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 20%, N-CTS2929 — 15% and J2 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 37%, K1a — 14% and U — 12%. YFull has 225 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is N-CTS2929: 15% on FTDNA against 23% on YFull.
