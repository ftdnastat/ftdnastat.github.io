Lithuania: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Lithuania: 1,857 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,063 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are N-FGC52464 — 13%, E-M34* — 4% and J1 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 14%, H1b — 5% and J1c — 5%. YFull has 225 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is N-FGC52464: 13% on FTDNA against 23% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
