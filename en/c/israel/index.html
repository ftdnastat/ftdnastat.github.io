Israel: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Israel: 244 kits with a Y-DNA result and 117 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 19%, E1b1b-M35 — 18% and R1a-Z2124 — 16%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 20%, L3 — 11% and U — 9%.
