Russia (Republic of Mordovia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Mordovia): 162 kits with a Y-DNA result and 8 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Mordovia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-CTS1211 — 21%, I1-DF29 — 15% and J2a — 10%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 69 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J2: 17% on FTDNA against 38% on YFull.
