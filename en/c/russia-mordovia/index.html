Mordovia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 190 kits with a Y-DNA result and 8 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Mordovia are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russia page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-CTS1211 — 23%, I1-DF29 — 14% and J2a — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 71 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J2: 16% on FTDNA against 38% on YFull.
