Russia (Republic of Mordovia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Mordovia): 157 kits with a Y-DNA result and 8 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are N-Tat* — 11%, R-Y2898 — 8% and I-S2077 — 8%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 69 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is J-Z1296: 6% on FTDNA against 20% on YFull.
