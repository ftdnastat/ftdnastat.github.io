Russia (Republic of Mordovia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Mordovia): 157 kits with a Y-DNA result and 8 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are N-Tat* — 11%, R-Y2898 — 8% and I-S2077 — 8%.
