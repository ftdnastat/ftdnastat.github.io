Romania: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Romania: 1,074 kits with a Y-DNA result and 557 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z2124 — 4%, J1 — 3% and J-YSC0000234* — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 14%, J1c — 7% and U5a — 4%. YFull has 154 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is I-Y3548: 3% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
