Romania: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Romania: 1,079 kits with a Y-DNA result and 555 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 15%, E1b1b-M35 — 15% and R1a-M198 — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 34%, K1 — 16% and U — 11%. YFull has 154 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is I: 15% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
