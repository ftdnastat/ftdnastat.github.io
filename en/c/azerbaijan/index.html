Azerbaijan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Azerbaijan: 607 kits with a Y-DNA result and 85 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J2a — 21%, J1 — 15% and R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 12%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 26%, U — 24% and J1 — 9%. YFull has 127 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E1b1b-M35: 8% on FTDNA against 15% on YFull.
