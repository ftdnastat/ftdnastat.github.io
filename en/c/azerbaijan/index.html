Azerbaijan: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Azerbaijan: 591 kits with a Y-DNA result and 85 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1-Z1842 — 7%, J2a-L25 — 6% and R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 12%, U1a — 6% and J1b — 5%. YFull has 127 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is E-PF6751: 1% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.
