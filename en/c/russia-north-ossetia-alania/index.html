North Ossetia-Alania: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 52 kits with a Y-DNA result. Only kits that name North Ossetia-Alania are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russia page. On the paternal line the most common are G2a1-Z6552 — 38%, J2a-Z6049 — 12% and G2a-U1 — 6%. YFull has 56 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is G2a1-Z6552: 38% on FTDNA against 48% on YFull.
