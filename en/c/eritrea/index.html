Eritrea: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Eritrea: 58 kits with a Y-DNA result and 20 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-Z830 — 43%, J1-FGC11 — 12% and A1b1 — 12%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 42 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E1b1b-M35: 57% on FTDNA against 71% on YFull.
