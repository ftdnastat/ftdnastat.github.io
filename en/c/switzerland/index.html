Switzerland: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Switzerland: 2,964 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,293 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-U152 — 8%, R-L2* — 5% and R1b-L151 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are T2b — 7%, J1c — 6% and K1a — 5%. YFull has 156 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
