Croatia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Croatia: 534 kits with a Y-DNA result and 298 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I2a-CTS10228 — 27%, R1a-CTS1211 — 16% and R1b-L23 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 19% and J1c — 6%. YFull has 152 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z282: 20% on FTDNA against 29% on YFull.
