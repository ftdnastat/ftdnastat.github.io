Croatia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Croatia: 516 kits with a Y-DNA result and 297 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-Y3548 — 23%, R-CTS3402* — 8% and R-Y2898 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 6%, U5b — 6% and U5a — 4%. YFull has 152 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-Y3548: 23% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
