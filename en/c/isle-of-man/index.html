Isle of Man: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Isle of Man: 143 kits with a Y-DNA result and 35 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-P312 — 41%, R1a-Z284 — 35% and I1-Z59 — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 35 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I1-Z59: 9% on FTDNA against 34% on YFull.
