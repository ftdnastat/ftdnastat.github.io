Algeria: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 506 kits with a Y-DNA result and 119 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are E1b1b-M35 — 34%, J1-FGC1707 — 30% and J2a — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 38%, U — 13% and L3 — 5%. YFull has 318 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E-PF2546: 20% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
