Algeria: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Algeria: 500 kits with a Y-DNA result and 117 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC7 — 23%, E-PF2546 — 20% and J-FGC23310* — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are H1 — 8%, H — 7% and L2a — 4%. YFull has 313 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E-PF2546: 20% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
