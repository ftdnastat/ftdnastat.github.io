Sudan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Sudan: 406 kits with a Y-DNA result and 53 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC23310* — 16%, A (tree root) — 9% and E-PF2179* — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are L2a — 23%, L0a — 11% and L3f — 11%. YFull has 146 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same.
