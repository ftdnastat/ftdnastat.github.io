Sudan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Sudan: 406 kits with a Y-DNA result and 52 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1-FGC1707 — 32%, E1b1b-M35 — 20% and T — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are L3 — 17%, T1a — 8% and L2a1d1 — 6%. YFull has 146 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC1707: 32% on FTDNA against 43% on YFull.
