Orstkhoy (Aosta Valley in FTDNA): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 192 kits with a Y-DNA result and 9 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are L-M357 — 59%, J2a-Z7671 — 16% and J1-CTS1460 — 14%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only.

Compare with…

Almost every kit filed under “Italy (Aosta Valley)” in FTDNA belongs to the Orstkhoy, a people closely related to the Chechens and Ingush. The Orstkhoy chose the Italian region because its flag resembles their own. There are practically no Italian lineages here, so this region is shown as Orstkhoy and detached from Italy in the statistics.
