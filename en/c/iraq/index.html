Iraq: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Iraq: 3,211 kits with a Y-DNA result and 144 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 34%, J2 — 22% and E1b1b-M35 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 24%, T2 — 15% and U — 10%. YFull has 892 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-P58: 27% on FTDNA against 39% on YFull.
