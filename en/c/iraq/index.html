Iraq: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Iraq: 3,166 kits with a Y-DNA result and 143 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-FGC4422 — 9%, J1 — 6% and J2 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are T2c — 11%, J1b — 8% and H — 7%. YFull has 892 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 4% on FTDNA against 8% on YFull.
