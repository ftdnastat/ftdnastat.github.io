France: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for France: 6,453 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,374 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 7%, R1b-U152 — 4% and R-L2* — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 5%, J1c — 5% and T2b — 5%. YFull has 433 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same.
