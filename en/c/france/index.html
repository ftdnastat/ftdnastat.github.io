France: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for France: 6,470 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,387 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 58%, I1 — 8% and J2 — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 45%, U — 12% and T2 — 8%. YFull has 433 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R: 66% on FTDNA against 60% on YFull.
