FamilyTreeDNA holds for Somalia: 136 kits with a Y-DNA result and 17 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-PF2179* — 60%, T-CTS931* — 13% and J-CTS9721* — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 125 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
