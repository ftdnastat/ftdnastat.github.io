Somalia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Somalia: 138 kits with a Y-DNA result and 19 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are E-V32 — 59%, T — 17% and E-Z830 — 2%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 125 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E-V32: 59% on FTDNA against 68% on YFull.
