Abkhazia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 95 kits with a Y-DNA result and 7 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Abkhazia are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Georgia page. On the paternal line the most common are G2a-L1264 — 23%, J2a — 18% and R1a-Z2124 — 14%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 80 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is G2a-L1264: 23% on FTDNA against 36% on YFull.
