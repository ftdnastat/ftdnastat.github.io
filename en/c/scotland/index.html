Scotland: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Scotland: 14,176 kits with a Y-DNA result and 4,612 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-S744 — 6%, R1b-L151 — 5% and R1b-L21 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 9%, U5a — 5% and T2b — 5%. YFull has 1,040 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I-L137: 2% on FTDNA against 5% on YFull.
