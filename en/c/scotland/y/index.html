Scotland Y-DNA

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Scotland: 14,194 kits with a Y-DNA result. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 67%, I1 — 11% and I2b-CTS616 — 4%. YFull has 1,040 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-L51: 67% on FTDNA against 56% on YFull.
