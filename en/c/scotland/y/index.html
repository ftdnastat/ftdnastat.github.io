Scotland Y-DNA

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Scotland: 14,181 kits with a Y-DNA result. On the paternal line the most common are R-S744 — 6%, R1b-L151 — 5% and R1b-L21 — 4%. YFull has 1,040 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I-L137: 2% on FTDNA against 5% on YFull.
