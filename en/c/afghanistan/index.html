Afghanistan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 167 kits with a Y-DNA result and 27 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z93 — 20%, G2b — 10% and J2 — 8%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 75 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z2125: 8% on FTDNA against 24% on YFull.
