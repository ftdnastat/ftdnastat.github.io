United States: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for United States: 30,879 kits with a Y-DNA result and 20,183 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 60%, I1-DF29 — 10% and I2 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 40%, U — 13% and T2 — 8%. YFull has 2,591 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1b-L51: 60% on FTDNA against 43% on YFull.
