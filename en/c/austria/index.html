Austria: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Austria: 1,085 kits with a Y-DNA result and 857 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 21%, R1a-M198 — 15% and E1b1b-M35 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 38%, U — 14% and K1 — 11%. YFull has 87 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I1: 8% on FTDNA against 1% on YFull.
