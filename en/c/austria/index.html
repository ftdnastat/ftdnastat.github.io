Austria: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Austria: 1,078 kits with a Y-DNA result and 857 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-M269 — 3%, I-Y3548 — 3% and J1 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 9%, J1c — 7% and T2b — 4%. YFull has 87 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is L-M22: 1% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.
