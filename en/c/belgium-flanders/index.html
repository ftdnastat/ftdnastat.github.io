Belgium (Flanders): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Belgium (Flanders): 98 kits with a Y-DNA result and 35 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Belgium (Flanders) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Belgium page. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-P312 — 33%, R1b-U106 — 30% and I1-DF29 — 14%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 58 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1b-U106: 30% on FTDNA against 12% on YFull.
