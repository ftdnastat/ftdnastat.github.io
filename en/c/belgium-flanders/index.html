Belgium (Flanders): summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Belgium (Flanders): 98 kits with a Y-DNA result and 35 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-L2* — 9%, I-Z2535 — 5% and R1b-Z18 — 5%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 58 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different.
