Belarus: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Belarus: 1,709 kits with a Y-DNA result and 738 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-M34* — 5%, R-Z92 — 4% and N-FGC52464 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 16%, J1c — 5% and HV1b — 5%. YFull has 328 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R-YP237: 2% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
