Belarus: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Belarus: 1,711 kits with a Y-DNA result and 738 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 23%, E1b1b-M35 — 13% and J2 — 13%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 30%, K1a — 16% and U — 12%. YFull has 328 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z282: 18% on FTDNA against 35% on YFull.
