Germany: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Germany: 17,561 kits with a Y-DNA result and 10,928 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 38%, I1 — 15% and R1a-M198 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 45%, U — 14% and T2 — 8%. YFull has 1,039 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-M269: 42% on FTDNA against 35% on YFull.
