Germany: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Germany: 17,540 kits with a Y-DNA result and 10,923 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 4%, I1 — 3% and R1b-U106 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 5%, K1a — 5% and U5a — 5%. YFull has 1,039 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-Z49: 1% on FTDNA against 4% on YFull.
