Morocco: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Morocco: 569 kits with a Y-DNA result and 168 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-PF2546 — 25%, J-FGC7 — 7% and E-PF2179* — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are H1 — 10%, H4a — 10% and L2a — 7%. YFull has 264 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E-PF2546: 25% on FTDNA against 39% on YFull.
