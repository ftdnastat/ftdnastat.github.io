Morocco: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Morocco: 568 kits with a Y-DNA result and 168 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E1b1b-M35 — 42%, J1-FGC1707 — 13% and J2 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 41%, U6a — 6% and HV — 6%. YFull has 264 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E1b1b-M35: 42% on FTDNA against 58% on YFull.
