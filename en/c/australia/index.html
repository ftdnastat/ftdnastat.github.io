Australia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Australia: 366 kits with a Y-DNA result and 378 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 6%, R1b-L21 — 6% and R-DF21* — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 7%, J1c — 5% and K1a — 4%. YFull has 99 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R-L20: 0% on FTDNA against 4% on YFull.
