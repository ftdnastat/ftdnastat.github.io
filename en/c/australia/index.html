Australia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 364 kits with a Y-DNA result and 378 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 60%, I1-DF29 — 11% and I2 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 36%, U — 15% and M — 7%. YFull has 99 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1b-L51: 60% on FTDNA against 46% on YFull.
