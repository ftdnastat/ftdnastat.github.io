Hungary: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Hungary: 1,920 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,093 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 22%, R1b-L51 — 15% and J2 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 38%, U — 15% and K1 — 8%. YFull has 282 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z282: 15% on FTDNA against 26% on YFull.
