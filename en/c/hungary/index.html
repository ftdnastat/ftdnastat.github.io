Hungary: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Hungary: 1,914 kits with a Y-DNA result and 1,093 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-Y3548 — 5%, J1 — 3% and R1a-CTS1211 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 7%, J1c — 6% and U5a — 5%. YFull has 282 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R-Y2898: 3% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.
