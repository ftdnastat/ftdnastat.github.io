England: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for England: 27,799 kits with a Y-DNA result and 12,369 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 56%, I1 — 15% and I2b — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 13% and T2 — 8%. YFull has 1,938 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is I1-DF29: 9% on FTDNA against 19% on YFull.
