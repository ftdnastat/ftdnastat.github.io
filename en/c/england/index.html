England: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for England: 27,739 kits with a Y-DNA result and 12,344 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 7%, I1 — 3% and R1b-U106 — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 7%, T2b — 5% and K1a — 5%. YFull has 1,938 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same.
