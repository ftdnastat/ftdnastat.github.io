Argentina: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 130 kits with a Y-DNA result and 100 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-P312 — 38%, R1b-U106 — 5% and J2a-M67 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are C1b — 19%, A2 — 16% and B2 — 16%.
