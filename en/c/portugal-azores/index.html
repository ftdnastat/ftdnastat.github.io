Portugal (Azores): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Portugal (Azores): 59 kits with a Y-DNA result and 40 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Portugal (Azores) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Portugal page. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-P312 — 36%, I1-Z59 — 7% and E-V13 — 7%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 214 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-DF27: 20% on FTDNA against 44% on YFull.
