FamilyTreeDNA holds for Chile: 164 kits with a Y-DNA result and 141 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-P312 — 38%, J2 — 7% and Q-M3 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are C1b — 23%, B2i2 — 22% and D1g — 18%.
