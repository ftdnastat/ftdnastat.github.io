Latvia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Latvia: 454 kits with a Y-DNA result and 261 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are N-CTS2929 — 22%, R1a-M198 — 19% and J2 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 37%, U — 14% and K1a — 11%. YFull has 78 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is N-CTS2929: 22% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
