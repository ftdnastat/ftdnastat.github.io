Latvia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Latvia: 452 kits with a Y-DNA result and 261 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are N-FGC52464 — 19%, R-CTS3402* — 4% and Q — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are K1a — 11%, J1c — 5% and H1b — 3%. YFull has 78 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is N-FGC52464: 19% on FTDNA against 40% on YFull.
