Canada (First Nations): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 50 kits with a Y-DNA result and 136 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). Only kits that name Canada (First Nations) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Canada page. On the paternal line the most common are C2-F1699 — 74%, R1b-P312 — 6% and I — 6%. On the maternal line the most common are A2 — 38%, C1 — 26% and D1 — 9%.
