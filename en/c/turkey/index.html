Turkey: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Turkey: 2,504 kits with a Y-DNA result and 896 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 8%, L-M22 — 5% and G-CTS574* — 3%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 6%, K1a — 6% and U3b — 4%. YFull has 528 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same.
