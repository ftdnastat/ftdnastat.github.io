Turkey: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Turkey: 2,523 kits with a Y-DNA result and 897 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J2a — 19%, J1 — 12% and R1b-Z2103 (M12149) — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 30%, U — 18% and J1 — 7%. YFull has 531 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is J1-P58: 6% on FTDNA against 9% on YFull.
