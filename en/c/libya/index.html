Libya: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Libya: 495 kits with a Y-DNA result and 28 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1-FGC1707 — 33%, E1b1b-M35 — 28% and J2 — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 164 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is E1b1b-M35: 28% on FTDNA against 48% on YFull.
