Serbia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Serbia: 350 kits with a Y-DNA result and 169 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are I2a-CTS10228 — 21%, R1a-Z282 — 12% and R1b-L23 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 43%, U — 22% and T2 — 5%. YFull has 357 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I2a-CTS10228: 21% on FTDNA against 35% on YFull.
