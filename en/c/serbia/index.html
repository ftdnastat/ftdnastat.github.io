Serbia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Serbia: 348 kits with a Y-DNA result and 166 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-Y3548 — 16%, NO — 7% and I-S20602* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 7%, J1c — 4% and K1a — 4%. YFull has 357 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is I-Y3548: 16% on FTDNA against 30% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
