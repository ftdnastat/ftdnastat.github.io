Ethiopia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Ethiopia: 74 kits with a Y-DNA result and 90 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E1b1b-M35 — 39%, E-V38 — 11% and J1-FGC11 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are L3 — 36%, L4 — 10% and L0a1 — 7%. YFull has 38 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is E-M78: 18% on FTDNA against 34% on YFull.
