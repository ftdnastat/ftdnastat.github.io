Ethiopia: summary

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Ethiopia: 74 kits with a Y-DNA result and 90 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are E-PF2179* — 12%, E-CTS10298* — 11% and J-FGC7638* — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are L3f — 9%, L0a — 8% and L4b — 7%. YFull has 38 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different.
