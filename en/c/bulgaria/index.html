Bulgaria: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Bulgaria: 884 kits with a Y-DNA result and 371 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-S20602* — 8%, I-Y3548 — 8% and E-BY3880* — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 6%, U5a — 5% and J1c — 5%. YFull has 227 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R-CTS7822: 4% on FTDNA against 7% on YFull.
