Bulgaria: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA has 885 kits with a Y-DNA result and 374 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are I2a-CTS10228 — 16%, J2 — 16% and E-V13 — 15%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 44%, U — 16% and T2 — 6%. YFull has 228 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R: 26% on FTDNA against 30% on YFull.
