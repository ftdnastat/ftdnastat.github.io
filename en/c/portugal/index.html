Portugal: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Portugal: 1,905 kits with a Y-DNA result and 904 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L151 — 11%, R-ZZ12_1* — 10% and E-PF2546 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 5%, K1a — 5% and U5b — 4%. YFull has 164 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same.
