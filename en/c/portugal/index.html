Portugal: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Portugal: 1,911 kits with a Y-DNA result and 905 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 48%, E1b1b-M35 — 13% and J2 — 8%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 39%, U — 12% and T2 — 6%. YFull has 164 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-P312: 33% on FTDNA against 41% on YFull.
