Slovakia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Slovakia: 980 kits with a Y-DNA result and 608 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 34%, R1b-L23 — 13% and I2a-CTS10228 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 43%, U — 16% and K1 — 8%. YFull has 103 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z282: 26% on FTDNA against 42% on YFull.
