Egypt: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Egypt: 617 kits with a Y-DNA result and 106 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J-YSC0000076 — 9%, J1 — 8% and E1b1b-M35 — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are L2a — 8%, L0a — 5% and L3f — 5%. YFull has 257 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J-FGC2: 2% on FTDNA against 5% on YFull.
