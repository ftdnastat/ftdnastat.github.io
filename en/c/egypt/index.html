Egypt: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Egypt: 619 kits with a Y-DNA result and 107 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are J1 — 38%, E1b1b-M35 — 23% and J2 — 10%. On the maternal line the most common are L3 — 17%, H — 16% and U — 10%. YFull has 257 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is J1-FGC11: 17% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
