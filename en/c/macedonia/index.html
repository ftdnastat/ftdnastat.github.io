Macedonia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Macedonia: 167 kits with a Y-DNA result and 76 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are J2 — 17%, E-V13 — 16% and I2a-CTS10228 — 16%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 46%, U — 21% and J1c2 — 7%. YFull has 36 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1b-L23: 10% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
