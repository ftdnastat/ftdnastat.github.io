Macedonia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Macedonia: 167 kits with a Y-DNA result and 76 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are I-S20602* — 10%, E-S2979 — 7% and J-Z1296 — 7%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 9%, H — 5% and U5a — 5%. YFull has 36 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R-CTS7822: 5% on FTDNA against 11% on YFull.
