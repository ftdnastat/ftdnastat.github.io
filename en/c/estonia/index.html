Estonia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Estonia: 293 kits with a Y-DNA result and 244 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are N-Y5004 — 14%, N-FGC52464 — 11% and N-L550* — 5%. On the maternal line the most common are U5a — 11%, H1a — 7% and J1c — 6%. YFull has 62 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is N-Y5004: 14% on FTDNA against 19% on YFull.
