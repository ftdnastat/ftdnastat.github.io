Estonia: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Estonia: 293 kits with a Y-DNA result and 245 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are N-CTS2929 — 32%, R1a-Z282 — 20% and I1-DF29 — 16%. On the maternal line the most common are H — 40%, U — 24% and J1 — 8%. YFull has 62 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R: 34% on FTDNA against 47% on YFull.
