Russia (Republic of Karachay-Cherkessia): haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Russia (Republic of Karachay-Cherkessia): 76 kits with a Y-DNA result and 11 kits with mtDNA. Only kits that name Russia (Republic of Karachay-Cherkessia) are counted here; those who gave just the country are on the Russian Federation page. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z2123 — 28%, G2a1-Z6552 — 25% and G2a-L1266 — 9%. For mt that is fewer than 50, so those shares below are a rough guide only. YFull has 112 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is G2a-L1264: 4% on FTDNA against 14% on YFull.
