Pakistan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Pakistan: 386 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z93 — 25%, J2 — 18% and R2 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are M — 31%, U7a3 — 9% and H — 9%. YFull has 142 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is Q: 9% on FTDNA against 18% on YFull.
