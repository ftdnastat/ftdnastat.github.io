Pakistan: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Pakistan: 382 kits with a Y-DNA result and 68 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R1a-Z94 — 14%, K-M526* — 11% and R1a-Z2124 — 9%. On the maternal line the most common are U7a — 12%, M5a — 6% and R5a — 6%. YFull has 140 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is R1a-Z2124: 9% on FTDNA against 14% on YFull.
