India: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for India: 1,485 kits with a Y-DNA result and 374 kits with mtDNA (mitochondrial DNA). On the paternal line the most common are R1a-M198 — 21%, J2 — 15% and R2 — 12%. On the maternal line the most common are M — 44%, R — 16% and U2 — 10%. YFull has 210 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is R1a-Z93: 13% on FTDNA against 28% on YFull.
