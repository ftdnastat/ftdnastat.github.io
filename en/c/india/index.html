India: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for India: 1,477 kits with a Y-DNA result and 372 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are GHIJK — 19%, K-M526* — 14% and R1a-Z94 — 11%. On the maternal line the most common are M5a — 5%, M30 — 5% and U2b — 4%. YFull has 210 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is almost the same. The largest gap is L-M22: 5% on FTDNA against 10% on YFull.

Detailed pages for each line are linked below the summaries.
