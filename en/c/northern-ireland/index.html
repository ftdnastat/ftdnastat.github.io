Northern Ireland: haplogroups

FamilyTreeDNA holds for Northern Ireland: 2,970 kits with a Y-DNA result and 614 kits with mtDNA. On the paternal line the most common are R-Z39589* — 5%, I-L137 — 5% and R-S588 — 4%. On the maternal line the most common are J1c — 9%, U5b — 4% and K1a — 4%. YFull has 240 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is noticeably different. The largest gap is I-CTS595: 1% on FTDNA against 6% on YFull.
