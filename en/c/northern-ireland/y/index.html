FamilyTreeDNA holds for Northern Ireland: 2,976 kits with a Y-DNA result. On the paternal line the most common are R1b-L51 — 74%, I2b-CTS616 — 7% and I1-DF29 — 6%. YFull has 240 samples for Y from the same area, and its mix of major branches is broadly the same. The largest gap is R1b-L51: 74% on FTDNA against 53% on YFull.
